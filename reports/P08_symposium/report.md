## Findings and intellectual rationale

### Build from documented strengths

The [CHEP overview](https://histecon.fr/en/) places legal/digital sources, economic/environmental history and economic ideas within its mission (P08-S01). Its collaboration began in 2019 and establishment followed in 2022. A fifth anniversary of establishment would therefore be in 2027, subject to institutional choice; this pilot does not require anniversary branding. Paris is the research boundary. Cambridge and Harvard are relevant through documented projects, not through an assumed unified membership or funding pool.

A practical asset is [Archives Notariales](https://www.histecon.fr/archivesnotariales/), a portal intended to support researchers and students, naming Claire Lemercier and David Todd among its contributors (P08-S08). Its [colonies and consulates page](https://www.histecon.fr/archivesnotariales/colonies.html) also illustrates why a portal cannot guarantee a ready digital dossier (P08-S09). Its undated digitisation statements were not independently updated. The event must use checked materials supplied by presenters, rather than promise easy access to any colonial source.

[Lemercier and Trivellato’s *1751 and Thereabout*](https://doi.org/10.1017/ssh.2022.8) directly supports a methodological emphasis on variation between notarial corpora (P08-PS16). The pilot extends that concern as an analytic proposal: an administrative category such as compensation cannot be transferred between cases without examining institutions, claimants and omissions. It should make disagreements visible rather than produce a harmonised dataset prematurely.

The junior-researcher connection is substantive. CHEP’s [2026 research-student page](https://histecon.fr/en/research_students.html) and the [28 January 2026 award notice](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ecole-recherche/en/news/four-young-history-researchers-chep-2026-grant/) document the students’ relationship for that year (P08-S14/PS07). They do not establish future participation, Master’s funding eligibility or money available for an event. Mathiot would be a co-presenter of a research problem; the organising student would prepare an analytical response and co-edit the outcome.

### Complement the calendar

The checked programmes show overlap risks, not a demonstrated shortage of events. Alongside the existing reading workshop, [La matière du politique](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/la-matiere-du-politique/) addresses materials in 2026–27 (P08-CAL-S04). The [LIEPP/CEE/TIERED planning workshop](https://www.sciencespo.fr/liepp/fr/actualites/what-is-economic-planning-historical-and-contemporary-perspectives/) is announced for 10–11 December 2026 (P08-CAL-S03). Neither should be represented as CHEP-organised without evidence.

Aesa Virely’s [administrative-archives session](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/mondes-sovietiques-mondes-communistes-et-post-communistes/) appears on 4 February under the 2026–27 heading; the year 2027 is inferred from that heading (P08-PS18). Balguy’s January 2026 source seminar is an indexed announcement whose direct page could not be opened (P08-PS20). Together these findings argue for a narrow, jointly worked exercise and an internal overlap check. A generic source seminar should not be advertised as a new unmet service.

No date is recommended from apparent gaps. The [regional agenda](https://histecon.fr/fr/agenda.html) supplied older discovery leads, while the checked PSE page returned a stale 2024 state (P08-CAL-S07/08). Confirm actual calendars, teaching obligations, examinations and room availability before selecting T. Listed events are announcements or institutional retrospective descriptions; attendance and impact were not verified.

## Three concepts and selection

The [concept notes](concepts.md) distinguish a methodological comparison (C1), a substantive historical problem about environmental loss (C2), and an emerging-project exchange about investment authority (C3).

| Criterion | C1: compensation records | C2: environmental loss | C3: investment authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intellectual coherence | High if both dossiers test claimant/proof categories | Strong question; scales and source forms may spread too widely | Clear tension between historical and normative approaches; needs careful scope |
| Community benefit | Junior co-presentation, shared annotation and practical feedback | Connects environmental and economic methods | Gives junior projects a structured encounter with senior arguments |
| Evidence of participant fit | Direct profiles plus comparative-method article | Direct environment profiles and joint programme | Direct fellowship/project evidence and coauthorship |
| Distinctiveness | Moderate: only the paired exercise differentiates it | Moderate/low: existing environmental programmes substantial | Low until nearby planning programme is assessed |
| Student workload | 29 hours for selected half-day | 29-hour half-day possible; fuller version 45 hours | Similar half-day envelope only after new confirmations; expansion unjustified now |
| Institutional dependencies | Two suitable dossiers, convenor, local support | Current local availability, cross-field convening | Senior availability and coordination with nearby work |
| Recommendation | **Prepare conditionally** | Reserve or adapt inside an existing seminar | Hold as a targeted follow-up possibility |

These qualitative judgements are **inferences**, not measured scores. Selection gives priority to a coherent research operation, meaningful student contribution and limited dependencies. Scholarly stature is not a ranking criterion. Interest in the format is untested.

C2 has a real network basis in [Economic History, Climate, and the Environment](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/ehc/) (P08-S10). C3 has a direct connection in [Cordelli and Levy’s *The Ethics of Global Capital Mobility*](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055421000964), online in September 2021 and in a May 2022 issue (P08-PS17). These alternatives remain useful if an academic convenor identifies demand; neither justifies an international meeting by itself.

## Decisions and evaluation

Three institutional decisions govern feasibility: whether the paired comparison adds scholarly value; who accepts academic and administrative responsibility; and whether resources and student allocation can be confirmed without displacing necessary access support. Future outreach and publication remain separate authorised actions. No messages, invitations, subscriptions or bookings were made during this run.

Evaluate discussion quality through specific revisions, not a satisfaction average alone. Proposed signals are two concrete changes to the dossier interpretations, visible student contributions and one voluntary continued research exchange. Use three optional feedback questions and one later aggregate check. Report response denominators and missing information; do not track private conversations. Continue only if participants describe useful scholarly changes and the convenor can sustain support. A well-documented decision not to repeat the format is a valid outcome. The assumed 12–16 places are a design parameter, not a success threshold or demand estimate.

## Methods and limitations

This independent P08 run read the complete shared brief, P08 and the nonempty four-link `Summary.md`; it did not depend on or execute other prompts. The corpus is a purposeful Paris-focused sample of profiles, projects, work evidence and 2025–2026 calendars, with announced 2026–27 programmes and selected earlier methodological anchors. It is not a membership or events census.

Navigation, language variants, aliases, archive links and targeted searches were checked; [coverage.md](coverage.md) records gaps. Browser results often expose cached crawl ages, so “verified” means supported by the inspected representation, not guaranteed live completeness. Roles, availability, event delivery and room charges require different evidence. Candidate records distinguish persons, placeholders and prospective institutional partners. [Source aliases](source_id_aliases.csv) reconcile repeated URLs from supporting notes without duplicating evidence. The estimates are explicit assumptions; demand, source rights and institutional capacity require internal information before implementation.
