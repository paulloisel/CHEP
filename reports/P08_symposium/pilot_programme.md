# P08 pilot — From compensation record to economic claim

as_of_date: 2026-11-15. **Unpublished, provisional discussion draft. No event, date, venue, participant or budget is confirmed.** Selected concept: P08-C1. All named candidates are uncontacted. Proposed times are Paris local time on an undecided date T; this is not a calendar booking.

## Purpose and evidence

Test one methodological question: **how does a record turn a loss into a recognised claim, and whom does that process exclude?** Candidate cases are colonial compensation to former slaveowners after abolition and recognition of maritime occupational risks. These are historically and morally distinct relationships. Similar documentary language does not make the injuries, entitlements or political purposes equivalent.

Fit draws on [Jessica Balguy’s CHSP profile](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/directory/balguy-jessica/) (P08-PS08), [Rébecca Mathiot’s doctoral profile](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/annuaire/mathiot-rebecca/) (P08-PS10), and [Lemercier and Trivellato’s comparative notarial article](https://doi.org/10.1017/ssh.2022.8) (P08-PS16). The 2026 CHEP relationship is supported by [Research students 2026](https://histecon.fr/en/research_students.html) (P08-S14). Source titles, dates and access dates are in [sources.csv](sources.csv), all accessed 15 November 2026. The proposed pairing is an **inference**; no existing collaboration or suitable ready-made dossier has been verified.

This format should first be discussed with an existing seminar convenor. [Histoires de la vie économique](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/) already offers shared reading (P08-CAL-S01). ## Capture template and outcomes

Use one shared worksheet per dossier with seven fields: **provenance; institutional purpose; claimant/unit; proof required; valuation category; exclusions/silences; unresolved question**. A second table compares the fields, with a mandatory “not comparable, and why” column. No personal details from unpublished cases enter the shared note without the presenter’s approval. A portal link is not permission to reproduce an archive image.

At T+1 week, the student drafts a two-page note: question and limits (one paragraph), paired source cards, two methodological disagreements, two possible next research steps. Presenters correct factual descriptions; the convenor checks historical framing. Attribute ideas only with contributors’ consent. Keep the working note within the agreed audience; wider publication requires a separate editorial decision. No edited collection, article, public database or recurring event is promised.

Optional evaluation, with no name requirement: (1) Which part changed or sharpened your interpretation? (2) What comparison was unhelpful or misleading? (3) Would a specific follow-up help your project? At T+4, make one optional aggregate check for a continued exchange, with no monitoring of individual conversations. Report the number responding and the denominator invited; absent replies remain missing. Proposed continuation signals are two concrete research revisions across the dossiers, useful student participation, and at least one voluntary follow-on exchange. These are learning criteria, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Public-facing copy — UNPUBLISHED AND PROVISIONAL

*From compensation record to economic claim* is a proposed CHEP source workshop exploring how historical documents define claimants, losses and evidence. Through two short dossiers, researchers and students would compare the making of economic claims while preserving the differences between colonial compensation and occupational protection. Participants would annotate sources, test interpretations and develop a shared methods note. The intended format gives students substantive presentation and response roles. Date, venue, contributors, language arrangements and participation conditions remain to be confirmed. This text is a discussion draft, not an invitation or event announcement.
