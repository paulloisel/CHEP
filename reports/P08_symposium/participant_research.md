## Recommended fit

The strongest small pilot compares two source dossiers: **how do records make an economic entitlement or loss visible?** Jessica Balguy and Rébecca Mathiot are plausible co-presenters, with Claire Lemercier as comparative-method respondent. Balguy studies compensation paid to former slaveowners after abolition and the subsequent history of colonial land; Mathiot studies recognition, prevention and compensation of maritime occupational risks. These are different historical relations and moral claims. The workshop must examine who made categories, whose claims were recorded and who was excluded; it must not equate compensation to slaveowners with protection of injured workers. That connection is an editorial inference, not a documented joint project. Public evidence does not establish that either has a suitable shareable dossier ready.
