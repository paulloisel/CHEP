## Boundaries, uncertainty and remaining retrieval queue

The inventory includes the specifically joint seminar from its earliest indexed session in May 2020, rather than importing pre-2020 Harvard-only seminars. It excludes Harvard tea talks, the Harvard graduate workshop, external conference talks by Cambridge/Harvard affiliates, and the Cambridge environmental reading group unless a separate event-specific Paris role is verified. Such exclusion guards against counting all network activity as Paris delivery. Wider network publicity may be catalogued separately in a communications exercise; it is outside this P04 core census.

Details still inaccessible after both canonical and www attempts: Diana Kim 2024 (`kim2.html`), Namrata Kala 2023 (`kala.html`), Nikolas Bowie 2021 (`bowie.html`), Jonathan Levy 2021 (`levy.html`), Paul Warde 2021 (`warde.html`), Sunil Amrith 2020 (`amrith.html`), Surabhi Ranganathan 2020 (`ranganathan.html`). Ogle's `ogle.html` is indexed with full text but cannot be opened directly. All remain represented using the successfully opened dated archive. Next step is an institutional archive or accessible dated snapshot for these pages, and an event owner check for the four early sessions lacking detail pages. No dated web-archive snapshots were recovered.

Current programme pages expose cached/indexed crawl dates ranging from weeks to years old. Crawl dates are retrieval metadata, never event or publication dates. Publication dates remain `date_unknown` where the page supplies none. Source register rows distinguish failed retrievals, indexed-only text and opened content. The current site itself was not independently fetched from the origin server, so this package establishes checked public evidence rather than real-time service availability.

Internal confirmation would settle the actual historical convenors; whether each announced session occurred; precise spoken languages and venues when unlisted; any additional or private sessions; and an agreed policy on whether network seminars belong in the headline CHEP event total or a separate subtotal. The strongest recommendation is to preserve that subtotal distinction and keep the event-status evidence field when reusing the inventory.
