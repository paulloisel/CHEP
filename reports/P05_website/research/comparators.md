# P05 comparator evidence memo

as_of_date: 2026-11-15  
Scope: selected public pages at three comparable scholarly centres/groups; website patterns only. This memo supports P05 and does not execute any other prompt. Access dates below are 2026-11-15. Summary.md and the complete shared brief were read.

## Retrieval qualification

All successful observations below come from the web retrieval tool, which exposed crawl-age labels. They verify what the retrieved representation contains, **not necessarily the current live rendering**. No browser-based usability or accessibility test was conducted for these comparators. Direct Python HTTP requests to three robots.txt files failed DNS resolution in the local environment; no automated crawl was undertaken. The Cambridge CHE 403/502/cache-miss results are retrieval failures, not verified broken public links.

## Conflicts and cautions

- Harvard's homepage representation exposed a 20 November 2026 event link, but its target [Future Events](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/events.htm) (P05-CMP-S03) returned an older crawled representation with a forthcoming-items placeholder. This is a **conflicting retrieval chain**, not proof of a present live-site defect. It supports testing the complete homepage-to-detail journey rather than merely counting links.
- The online-seminar search result said the 2026–27 programme would be posted later, while the opened [seminar page](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/seminars/index.html) (P05-CMP-S04) announced an October 2026 programme. Prefer the opened representation for this memo and retain the cache limitation; neither establishes the next future Paris event on 15 November.
- CAMPOP's retrieved homepage retains a 60th-anniversary statement alongside dated 2026 news, while its institutional history in the same sentence gives 1964. This illustrates why anniversary copy should use an explicit year, but this memo does not diagnose the live page as stale. [CAMPOP home](https://www.campop.geog.cam.ac.uk/) (P05-CMP-S08).
- CAMPOP's extracted seminar text includes literal HTML-like fragments and repeated headings. Their presence in the retrieval does not by itself establish what a browser or screen reader displays. The useful comparator is the information distinction, not an endorsement of its implementation.
- The [Cambridge CHE homepage](https://www.histecon.magd.cam.ac.uk/) (P05-CMP-S11) was attempted, including non-www and index.html variants, plus seminars and the historical /chep/ branch. These were inaccessible to this tool. CAMPOP was therefore used as a third inspected comparator. It is a different scholarly group, not a substitute authority on CHEP's organisation.

## Suggested acceptance checks for CHEP

1. A visitor can find a dated future activity or an accurately dated no-confirmed-listing statement within two links from the homepage. This is a proposed criterion, not measured performance.
2. From any sampled archive page, the visitor can find current listings without returning to a search engine.
3. A funding or participation page states who is eligible and whether an action is currently open, closed or awaiting confirmation; it does not use an archive as a current call.
4. The public page distinguishes Paris, Harvard, Cambridge and joint ownership at the item level.
