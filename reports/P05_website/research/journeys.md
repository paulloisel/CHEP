## Content issues and restraint

1. **Role and date clarity.** The host landing identifies Jonathan Levy as coordinator. Its community includes Jessica Balguy and Joan Chaker as postdoctoral researchers. The CHEP fellows page contains Jessica Balguy only; root confirmed both pages live (`host-en-alias-ax.txt`, `fellows-en-ax.txt`). This is an unresolved difference in scope, dates or maintenance, not proof that either person is incorrectly listed. Confirm roster responsibility and affiliation periods before reconciling. [Sources J-S03/J-S09 below.]
2. **Useful existing researcher depth.** Balguy's substantive biography and Giosuè's student profile already make research intelligible. Adapt these into consistent cards rather than replacing useful long-form content with photographs alone. [J-S03/J-S06.]
3. **Publications page scope.** The heading states recent publications by participants; avoid changing it to a claim of exhaustive CHEP-produced or CHEP-funded research. French and English indexed listings carry the same 18 titles. Absence of a date on the listing is not evidence the book is recent. Use metadata from publishers. [J-S07/J-S08/J-S10.]
4. **Historical depth is valuable.** Events_recent includes material back to 5 June 2019; the graduate visitor page labels 2021–2022; past fellows records periods. Preserve these resources with explicit archive labels and durable links. [J-S13/J-S17/J-S18.]
5. **Archive chronology requires a check.** Events_recent labels “Belonging, membership, affiliation” 18–19 September 2023 while its description calls it the second stage after a September 2023 Cambridge event. The workshop introduction repeats the relationship but does not state a date in extracted body text. Programme retrieval failed. Flag a date query rather than substituting a guessed year. [J-S13/J-S19/J-S20.]
6. **Language context.** The Sciences Po CHEP page's language link points to the CHSP French home, not an equivalent CHEP page; root's live `host-en-alias-ax.txt` confirms this destination, so context loss is a supported navigation concern. Indexed CHEP French contact includes Twitter and older-looking text; English includes Bluesky. Because the French cache was labelled 2.6 years old, this does not establish a current live mismatch. [J-S09/J-S11/J-S12/J-S21.]
7. **URL preservation.** The observed Rébecca Mathiot profile link includes an encoded space before `.html`: `https://histecon.fr/en/Rebecca_Mathiot%20.html`. It failed indexed retrieval. Preserve the exact known URL in a migration map and test a redirect only after webmaster verification; do not declare it broken or silently normalise it. [J-S05/J-S22.]

## Researcher prototype source option

[Jonathan Levy's Sciences Po profile](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/researcher/Jonathan%20Levy/89581.html) (J-S23) states Professor, CHSP, with research interests in US capitalism, money and climate. Its biography describes research on climate change in Houston and a global history of money; it lists The Real Economy (2025), Ages of American Capitalism (2021) and Freaks of Fortune (2012). This can ground a short profile template together with CHEP's organisation page. Do not infer CHEP funding for those books, a start date for the coordinator role, or an approved visitor/supervision offer. A source-linked institutional profile is the safer route for professional enquiries than copying personal data into new directories.

## Method and remaining checks

Targeted searches used `site:histecon.fr "Histories of Economic Life" "2026"`, `site:histecon.fr "research fellows"` and `site:sciencespo.fr/histoire "CHEP" "Levy"`. Opened underlying results used in claims; search leads not opened were excluded. All six required tasks were considered; the final event and subscription results incorporate root's live browser checks. Registration and subscription submissions were deliberately outside scope. No external author-profile census, full event chronology, publisher census, download of copyrighted books, formal accessibility check, application-portal test, or participant study was attempted. Confirm actual event participation conditions and the owner of content corrections internally; no commitment is assigned to named staff.
