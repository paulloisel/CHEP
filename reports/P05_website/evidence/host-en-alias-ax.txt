It regularly convenes events on fundamental issues, including the relationship between history and economics, the dialogue between economic and environmental history, the use of sources (including digital and visual sources) by historians and economists, and the history of economic ideas and experiences. CHEP is devoted to encouraging work on the history of economic life—broadly construed—by doctoral students using a wide variety of research perspectives and methods.
