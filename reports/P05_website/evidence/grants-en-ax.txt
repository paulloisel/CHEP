In collaboration with the Centre for History and Economics in Cambridge and the Joint Center for History and Economics in Harvard, CHEP encourages historical research on all aspects of economic life. It is especially interested in the dialogue between economic and environmental history, the use of legal and digital sources by economists and historians, and the history of economic ideas and experiences. The successful candidates will participate in activities organised by CHEP and obtain access to up to 1000€ in research expenses (e.g. transport and accommodation costs of a research trip, participation in a conference, language learning, etc.) in the calendar year 2027. PhD students from all research centres at Sciences Po, in their first, second or third year are eligible. Applicants need to provide:
