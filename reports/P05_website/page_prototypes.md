#### Historical research on economic life

The Centre for History and Economics in Paris (CHEP) brings an international, multidisciplinary perspective to the history of economic life. Based at the Sciences Po Centre for History, it was established in 2022 following a collaboration with the Cambridge–Harvard Joint Centre for History and Economics that began in 2019. Its activities connect economic and environmental history, historical approaches to legal and digital sources, and the history of economic ideas and experiences. CHEP also contributes to the shared programme Economic History, Climate, and the Environment. Explore the people, projects and publications below, or consult the practical information for a forthcoming activity. Participation arrangements are provided with individual events and calls.
