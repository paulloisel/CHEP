# P12 comparator research notes

as_of_date: 2026-11-15. Access date for every source: 2026-11-15. Scope: five selected official examples, not a census or ranking. These are transferable mechanisms, not evidence that CHEP lacks them. No invitation, registration, application or subscription was sent.

## Findings for the programme designer

1. **Make admission explicit.** [IHR History Lab](https://www.history.ac.uk/about-us/history-lab) states free membership for MA, MRes, MPhil and PhD students in history or related subjects; it describes postgraduate organisation and short research pitches (P12-C001). Transfer the clarity and short introduction, not the scale or assumed reach. A CHEP welcome page must state locally confirmed permissions rather than importing this eligibility.
2. **Give feedback a named role and time allocation.** The [EHS 2024 online workshop](https://ehs.org.uk/event/third-online-phd-thesis-workshop/) specifies presentations, ten-minute discussant feedback and questions (P12-C002). Its [2021 reflection](https://ehs.org.uk/reflections-on-the-economic-history-societys-inaugural-phd-thesis-workshop/) documents circulation before the meeting (P12-C003). Borrow a modest excerpt and rotating discussant; these are two historical editions, not a verified 2026 open programme.
3. **Allow genuinely unfinished material.** [CERI’s Atelier doctoral sur la Chine contemporaine](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ceri/fr/content/atelier-doctoral-sur-la-chine-contemporaine) accepts research at different stages and circulates working documents before group discussion (P12-C004). Adapt an optional two-page source/problem note. Doctoral access in the comparator does not establish Master's access at either institution.
4. **Combine researcher coordination with academic backing.** The [EUI Intellectual History Working Group](https://www.eui.eu/research-hub?id=intellectual-history) names researcher coordinators and a supporting professor; its format combines introductions with discussion and visiting scholars (P12-C005). This supports a role design, not copying a fortnightly frequency or assuming EUI visitors are available to Paris.
5. **Cost logistical help explicitly.** Cambridge’s [October 2026 AHSS bulletin](https://www.ahssresearch.group.cam.ac.uk/ahss-research-and-impact-bulletin) describes CRASSH Connections with up to GBP 250 per event plus conditional booking/publicity support; convenors manage hybrid technology (P12-C006). This is a documented allocation ceiling, not actual expenditure. A CHEP adaptation should reuse existing seminar space and platforms, and put introductions within an existing occasion before creating a separate event.

## Coverage, status and conflicts

Searches used IHR/History Lab, EHS thesis workshop, Sciences Po atelier doctoral, EUI intellectual history group, and CRASSH network/support terms. Search-only leads (LinkedIn, Wikipedia, AEHN newsletters and broader funding calls) were not adopted as evidence. Web open results exposed crawl metadata: IHR and Cambridge bulletin "today"; EHS 2024 and CERI "2 months ago"; EUI "last month"; EHS reflection "3 months ago". Therefore these are inspected indexed/cached official-page representations, not live-site uptime tests. Source publication dates are unknown unless an explicit dated article or bulletin is identified. No archive-snapshot dates were provided.

The CERI search result said Inalco/Sciences Po and one presenter; the opened page said EHESS/Sciences Po and two presenters. Both agreed on doctoral focus, advance circulation, multiple stages and group discussion. Retain those common mechanisms; co-organising institution and number of presenters are **conflicting** and excluded from transfer claims pending a live check. Likewise, the EHS 2024 headline/time block and programme contain timing/count inconsistencies, so no participant count or exact total duration is adopted. The programme's feedback roles are explicit.

Resource levels are compared through *documented operating arrangements*: postgraduate-led network, student trustee-led society workshop, doctoral convenors, researcher group with academic support, and a cash-backed centre service with administration. Absolute budgets and paid staffing of the first four remain not_found_publicly; do not call them underfunded or zero-cost. No causal evaluation of effectiveness was conducted.
