## Institutional boundary

CHEP is based at the Centre d’histoire de Sciences Po (CHSP). The [Sciences Po overview](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/research/centre-for-history-and-economics-in-paris-chep/) distinguishes 2019 collaboration from 2022 establishment (P12-N001). [Organisation](https://histecon.fr/en/organisation.html) labels Jonathan Levy **Coordinator**, alongside separate coordination and web functions (P12-S003). The other centres are collaborators, not subordinate departments. This pilot asks for support by function and assigns no individual a commitment.

## Gaps as testable hypotheses

- **P12-H01:** clearer entry information would help. Test by asking a newcomer to locate one suitable activity, its access route and preparation instructions; record unclear steps without individual tracking.
- **P12-H02:** a short feedback opportunity would add value beyond existing provision. Ask first what people already use; if it is sufficient, drop the sessions.
- **P12-H03:** an opt-in resource exchange would sustain a useful conversation. Test through anonymous later-session reports, not claims of new partnerships.
- **P12-H04:** a light maintenance task fits available support. Log student and counterpart time separately; stop or reduce delivery if capacity is exceeded.

The needs instrument and decision rules are in [needs_assessment.md](needs_assessment.md). These hypotheses require internal information; public research alone cannot resolve them.
