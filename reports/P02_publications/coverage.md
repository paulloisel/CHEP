## File conventions and validation

CSV files are UTF-8 with quoted fields where necessary. Semicolons separate source/person IDs and thematic tags. Missing data use `not_found_publicly`, `date_unknown`, `not_applicable` or explicit `not_assessed` labels. A work's earliest known publication year can precede the catalogued edition; dates preserve known precision rather than inventing January 1. `canonical_url` is unknown where only a bibliography/container was recovered. `evidence_url`, `source_title` and `source_ids` preserve support. IDs are local stable identifiers, not invented DOI/ISBN/ORCID values.

`verification_status` qualifies bibliographic metadata, not the validity of every institutional relationship. The 16 unverified rows still have author-list evidence of existence but uncertain material detail; do not silently cite all 188 as fully verified outputs. `count_in_published_text_total=yes` identifies the inclusive, provisional text catalogue, while supplementary rows are always `no`. Filter on `verification_status=verified` for the narrower 170-record view. No images or copyrighted full texts are reproduced.

Automated checks verified unique record IDs, resolved source/person references, exactly 18 featured rows/14 featured work IDs, valid evidence statuses and matching author incidence counts. An independent review checked duplicates, future dates and reconciliation of all fellow entries. The five multi-record work groups are four translation pairs and one book/excerpt pair. The report's type, cohort and period totals are generated from the final CSVs, not summed from author totals.
