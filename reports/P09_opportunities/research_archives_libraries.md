## Findings for the decision brief

There are **seven retained schemes** in this component: three open, three recurring watchlist entries, and one closed call. The two Newberry programmes have genuinely different degree and duration rules. The two Hagley grants distinguish exploratory from extended archival work. Individual named Newberry/Huntington funds were not inflated into separate opportunities.

- **Urgent:** the Huntington short-term competition remains open on 15 November until **23:59 PST**, including its reference. This is useful only if the dossier and referee are already ready. The official category is “graduate students”; applying this category to a French Master's student is an educational-stage mapping, not an explicit French degree equivalence. No ABD prerequisite is stated. [Fellowships Program](https://www.huntington.org/research-and-fellowships/fellowships-program), [FAQ](https://www.huntington.org/research-and-fellowships/fellowship-faqs) (P09-SA08, SA10).
- **Advanced doctoral and later researchers:** Newberry short-term support has a December application deadline and separate later referee cutoff. Long-term support requires a completed doctorate and has a November deadline. Neither should be advertised as a general Master's option. [Short-Term Fellowships](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/short-term-fellowships), [Long-Term Fellowships](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/long-term-fellowships), [How to Apply](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/how-to-apply) (P09-SA01–03).
- **Master's preparation pipeline:** Hagley allows broad applicant access but its inspected pages give standing dates without a year; the conservative directory treatment is watchlist, not an invented 2027 deadline. Sørensen explicitly welcomed Master's students in the last call; “early 2027” is announced for the next call, but no exact opening or deadline was retrieved. [Hagley Grants & Fellowships](https://www.hagley.org/research/grants-fellowships?language=en), [Sørensen programme](https://www.eui.eu/en/academic-units/historical-archives-of-the-european-union/research-support/postgraduate-vibeke-soerensen-grant), [2026 call](https://www.eui.eu/news-hub?id=call-for-applications-the-vibeke-soerensen-grant-programme-2026&lang=en-GB) (P09-SA05, SA12–13).
- **Paris-based watchlist:** BnF's Hyman call is closed. It is retained because its domestic-economy/print-history theme and M2-compatible rules make it a useful targeted lead. The amount is a total award, not an annual wage; doctoral-contract restrictions matter. Future continuation of this particular new named award was not verified. [Hyman call](https://bnfaac2026.sciencescall.org/page/bourse_hyman), [2026–27 general call](https://bnfaac2026.sciencescall.org/), [regulation](https://bnfaac2026.sciencescall.org/data/pages/Reglement_de_l_appel_a_chercheurs_et_chercheuses_2026_2027.pdf) (P09-SA14–17).

## Scope, exclusions and remaining work

This component covers selected publicly documented archive/library support for prospective Paris-associated applicants. It is not a census of all archival fellowships. Selection prioritised a defensible 2027 cycle, economic/social/print-history utility, and degree-stage diversity. All seven retained records have degree, affiliation, geography, money, timing, materials, nomination and funding-combination fields; undisclosed conditions remain explicit missing values.

Excluded from the directory: Huntington travel/exchange grants requiring applicants based in North America; Newberry consortium-only awards without verified Sciences Po membership; BnF unfunded associated-researcher status as if it were cash funding; unsupported next-year deadlines. IISH searches did not produce a sufficiently checked current call within this focused pass; its thematic Iran fellowships remain a retrieval lead, not a directory record. Other BnF thematic branches were visible but not systematically extracted: those are a future expansion queue only if relevant projects emerge. No award, application, host acceptance or willingness was inferred for any CHEP member.

One source-level discrepancy remains: Sørensen admission text excludes current EUI scholars, but the same programme's 2026 recipients include an EUI doctoral affiliation. The published admission rule remains the directory rule; the recipient listing does not establish an exception available to new applicants. For the present Paris scope this normally matters only if an applicant also has EUI affiliation.

Huntington's public programme pages and portal give inconsistent approximate short-term award counts (140 versus 120); this has no bearing on the verified amount, eligibility or deadline, and no competition-wide success probability is offered. The short-term Master’s mapping and the equivalence of a French doctoral stage to Newberry's ABD should be checked for an actual applicant. The directory must not imply that merely identifying as a CHEP affiliate meets a degree requirement.

The student can next prepare a collection-specific proposal template and budget-gap worksheet. Before endorsing any application, the applicant—not the directory—must establish registration/degree stage, actual residence, ability to take research leave, existing awards and visas. No contacts or applications were made.
