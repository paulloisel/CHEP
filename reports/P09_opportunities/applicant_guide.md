# P09 — Five applicant selection sheets

**As of 15 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles.** Each numbered sheet is designed as a standalone one-page guide. All calls were accessed on that date; source titles, dates and retrieval limitations are in [sources.csv](sources.csv). Match the IDs to [opportunities.csv](opportunities.csv) for complete terms and [deadline_calendar.md](deadline_calendar.md) for application versus referee dates. These are selection aids, not findings about any named person's eligibility.

## Sheet 1 — Master's student

**Start with your activity:** a short archive trip, a credited study/internship mobility, or a methods workshop. Different routes pay for different things. There ARE currently open Master's-relevant schemes in the checked corpus; there is no verified universal entitlement based on CHEP association.

**Check first:** RHS postgraduate research support (**O018**) if already a paid postgraduate member studying a research Master's in History, with no previous grant under the scheme. Confirm that your preparation meets its expectations; an M1 applicant should not assume stage requirements are automatic. The December call covers research expenses, not conference attendance. Joining now is not a reliable way into this round. [RHS call](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/); [membership process](https://royalhistsoc.org/membership/membership-postgraduate/) (P09-SS01–02).

**Check next:** CIERA's January workshop (**O009**) if you can discuss research in both French and German at B1 or above and have a suitable text. It funds participation costs; Paris residents do not get travel reimbursement. Erasmus (**O003**) is relevant only if your school approves compulsory credited mobility of at least two months. [CIERA workshop](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/24827); [Sciences Po Erasmus+](https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/fr/financer/bourses-aides-financieres/programme-erasmus-plus/).

**Plan ahead:** CIERA Master's research (**O005**) has an announced April deadline; resolve its inconsistent French residence/enrolment conditions. Hagley (**O013–014**) and Sørensen (**O016**) belong on the watchlist. Huntington (**O015**) closes tonight: graduate-category eligibility maps provisionally to a Master's, but only an already prepared collection-specific dossier is realistic.

**Private facts to establish:** M1/M2 and research-degree status; membership; residence history; languages; credited mobility; destination and dates; other awards; ability to advance costs. CHEP studentships and Newberry short-term funding are not Master's routes. Next useful output: one paragraph connecting your sources to a trip, with an itemised cost gap.

## Sheet 2 — Early doctoral student

**Check first:** CHEP (**O001**) if registered at Sciences Po in doctoral year 1, 2 or 3. The fund is open across Sciences Po research centres, but the proposal must connect to CHEP's research priorities. Do not substitute informal participation for enrolment. [CHEP Research grants](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html) (P09-SR01).

**Match the expense:** a language course, archive trip or conference may fit CHEP. Research visits/reproduction may fit RHS postgraduate funding (**O018**) for existing eligible members. A conference is expressly outside the RHS grant's scope. EHS posters (**O022**) offer a presentation route if you meet the doctoral-stage rules; the possible attendance contribution excludes travel and has no published bursary deadline. [RHS call](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/); [EHS New Researcher Posters](https://ehs.org.uk/conference/new-researcher-posters/).

**Prepare later:** CIERA distinguishes researchers without a research allowance (**O006**) from those with doctoral funding (**O007**). The destination, project and French residence/enrolment rules apply in addition to degree stage. An announced deadline is not evidence the next form is open. The Sciences Po exceptional doctoral fund (**O002**) is a local monitoring route. [CIERA complete aid](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/12187); [partial aid](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/12188).

**Stop checks:** the spring-2027 doctoral Erasmus competition (**O004**) has already closed in Paris time. The EHA early-stage grant (**O024**) has conflicting call/form availability and is not ready to apply. Newberry short-term normally needs ABD equivalence, so do not spend time on it before that stage.

**Private facts to establish:** degree year at the required reference date; funding/contract; precise research costs; referee availability; other reimbursement sources. Next output: the CHEP checklist below, if eligible, with a draft statement and CV. Do not book non-refundable travel on a prospective award.

## Sheet 3 — Advanced doctoral student

**Check first:** Newberry short-term (**O011**) when its collections are central to the project and your supervisor can establish ABD-equivalent status. Generic interest in a US visit is insufficient. A proposal, collection list and reference are distinct work items. [Newberry Short-Term Fellowships](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/short-term-fellowships); [application instructions](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/how-to-apply) (P09-SA01, SA03).

**Check next:** RHS postgraduate funding (**O018**) can support a smaller targeted research expense if your membership and prior-award history fit. CIERA (**O006–007**) can support eligible fieldwork, not simply writing up the dissertation. Its FAQ permits later doctoral applications but expects a convincing explanation of progress and the necessity of remaining research. [RHS call](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/); [CIERA FAQ](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/node/12977).

**Consider a longer commitment only if it fits:** Harvard IRIS (**O023**) is for advanced social-science research on international relations/security and requires campus residence. EHS/IHR (**O021**) can suit someone submitting the thesis by the fellowship-year threshold; British mentorship and attendance must be feasible. Neither is an entitlement from CHEP's partnerships. [Harvard call](https://academicpositions.harvard.edu/postings/16700); [EHS/IHR portal](https://universityoflondon.smapply.io/prog/ihr_economic_history_society_fellowship_2027/).

**Private facts to establish:** thesis progress and expected submission; whether a doctorate will be awarded before the relevant cutoff; host approval; leave from employment; existing awards and permitted overlap; dependent costs and residence feasibility. For a D4-or-later student, CHEP's D1–D3 rule is a hard barrier; inspect the School's wider fund instead.

Next output: a collection- or programme-specific two-page feasibility note and timeline. Agree with your supervisor whether a month away advances completion or adds delay before preparing a full fellowship dossier.

## Sheet 4 — Postdoctoral researcher

**Check first for a small project:** RHS early-career support (**O019**) if you are a current paid member within its corrected-doctorate age rule, accounting for permitted career interruptions, and have not held this award before. It is for research costs, not salary or conference organisation. [Early Career Research Support Grants](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/early-career-research-funding/early-career-research-support-grants/) (P09-SS04).

**Check first for a substantial fellowship:** EHS/IHR (**O021**) has a clear economic/social-history fit and does not require British affiliation already in hand at application. It does require a suitable British mentor during the award, IHR participation and compliance with limits on other maintenance grants and paid work. Resolve the conflicting closing times and what counts as a recent doctorate. [EHS fellowships](https://ehs.org.uk/award/economic-history-society-fellowships/); [IHR portal](https://universityoflondon.smapply.io/prog/ihr_economic_history_society_fellowship_2027/).

**For collection-led research:** Newberry short-term (**O011**) and long-term (**O012**) suit different durations. The latter has an imminent deadline and excludes another fellowship during residence. Huntington (**O015**) is only a same-day option for an already complete dossier. For later planning, CIERA young-doctor aid (**O008**) has a four-year PhD-age condition and French degree/contract plus residence requirements. [Newberry long-term](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/long-term-fellowships); [CIERA young doctors](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/12975).

**Private facts to establish:** PhD date and interruptions; current employment; leave and salary continuation; institutional affiliation; actual residence; overlapping funding; collections and visa feasibility. A postdoctoral job title does not determine all these facts.

Next output: compare one short visit against one residential option using total personal costs, foregone salary if applicable, payment timing and expected research progress. A larger award need not be the more practical choice.

## Sheet 5 — Other affiliate: visiting, independent or established researcher

**Start with your real status:** “CHEP affiliate” is not a grant degree category. A visiting or independent researcher may qualify for an external collection grant without qualifying for Sciences Po student funds. A senior scholar is not an early-career applicant merely because their affiliation began recently.

**Check collection routes:** Newberry permits applicants without institutional affiliation, but its degree rules still apply: short-term ABD/PhD, long-term completed PhD. Hagley's exploratory and du Pont grants (**O013–014**) have broad applicant access and are worth monitoring for business/economic-history research; no dated next round is entered. [Newberry application instructions](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/how-to-apply); [Hagley Grants & Fellowships](https://www.hagley.org/research/grants-fellowships?language=en) (P09-SA03, SA05).

**For collaboration:** an eligible current RHS member could investigate a one-day workshop (**O020**). Paris venue eligibility is not explicit and must be clarified before presenting the proposal as ready. The budget cannot pay for the organiser's work. A CIERA junior-conference team (**O010**) is a closed-round model for doctoral/recent-postdoctoral collaboration, not an open general-affiliate grant. [RHS Workshop Grants](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/workshop-grants/rhs-workshop-grants/); [CIERA call](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/24218).

**Private facts to establish:** highest completed degree; PhD age if relevant; employment/retirement and leave; membership; institutional accounts able to administer funds; residence; source collections; ability to travel. If you also meet a student or postdoctoral profile, use that sheet's precise conditions.

Next output: a one-page project and cost case, with a named institutional function that could receive/administer an award where required. Do not presume CHEP can advance costs or provide a host nomination. Historical Harvard/Cambridge collaboration programmes are not evidence that a current competition exists.

## Preparation checklist A — CHEP 2027 studentship (O001)

Based on [CHEP Research grants](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html), P09-SR01. This checklist helps an eligible doctoral applicant prepare; it is not an application submission.

- [ ] Confirm Sciences Po registration and D1–D3 eligibility; clarify which date determines the year if uncertain.
- [ ] Choose a research activity in calendar 2027. Draft a realistic expense list, within the €1,000 ceiling, and identify any other reimbursement sources. A budget is recommended preparation, not an extra published application requirement.
- [ ] Draft the required research statement, maximum two pages, explaining the connection to CHEP priorities; attach a current CV. French or English is accepted.
- [ ] Identify a referee and, in the applicant's own later correspondence, obtain agreement to provide a letter. This research run has not contacted anyone.
- [ ] Verify the linked application route manually. Tool retrieval failed; exact cutoff time/timezone were not published in the inspected call.
- [ ] **Suggested internal target 7 December:** submit the complete application early enough to trigger the automatic referee request. Official application deadline: **11 December 2026**.
- [ ] Track the reference separately; official reference deadline: **15 December 2026**. Suggested reminder target: 10 December. Do not assume uploading the application also uploads a letter.
- [ ] Retain the submission receipt and the version of the call used. If awarded, clarify eligible spending, receipts, reimbursement and permitted funding overlap before incurring costs.

Estimated applicant preparation: **4–6 hours** with an existing project/CV, plus referee work. This is an estimate, not a service commitment.

## Preparation checklist B — RHS postgraduate research grant (O018)

Based on [Postgraduate Research Support Grants](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/) and [Postgraduate Membership](https://royalhistsoc.org/membership/membership-postgraduate/), P09-SS01–02. This is the Master's-compatible checklist; doctoral applicants can use it too.

- [ ] Confirm a research Master's/PhD in History, current paid postgraduate membership, no previous award under this scheme and adequate research preparation.
- [ ] Stop if not already eligible through membership. The published new-membership process does not support an assumption of admission before this grant deadline.
- [ ] Select future research costs within the call's categories. Separate conference fees, equipment purchases and salary, which this scheme excludes.
- [ ] Prepare the required **maximum 500-word proposal** and itemised, justified budget. Explain why the archive/source work is necessary and what it enables.
- [ ] Identify an academic referee. The applicant should arrange the letter; the directory operator should not collect confidential references.
- [ ] **Suggested target 27 November:** have the reference uploaded. Unlike CHEP, the portal does **not permit applicant submission until the reference is received**.
- [ ] **Suggested target 2 December:** final check and submission; the official deadline is **4 December 2026**, time/timezone unstated. Do not plan on a separate later referee window.
- [ ] Declare other funding as required. Additional support is not an automatic exclusion, but financial need affects priority; do not claim the same cost twice without permission.
- [ ] Save confirmation and await the decision before relying on the award. The funding requested is £500 or £1,000 according to the proposed activity; it is not guaranteed.

Estimated applicant preparation: **3–5 hours** for a mature project plus referee work and any access arrangements. The two checklists intentionally expose opposite reference workflows so applicants do not transfer the wrong procedure between funders.
