## Decision brief

**What we learned.** Funding is available, but CHEP association is not itself an eligibility category. The strongest immediate local route is the **2027 CHEP studentship for Sciences Po doctoral years 1–3**. Master's students need separate routes: RHS research support for eligible existing members, CIERA's subsidised methods workshop, and approved Erasmus mobility are materially different opportunities. Archive fellowships depend on collections and degree stage. The directory contains **24 entries: 12 open, four with announced future dates, four recurring without a dated next round, three closed, and one with unverified availability**. “Open” includes an ongoing administrative route and a conditional conference-bursary inquiry; it never means everyone can apply. [CHEP call](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html); [RHS postgraduate grants](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/); [CIERA workshop](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/24827); [directory](opportunities.csv).

**What CHEP can do.** Offer a small, checked selection service that supplements the School of Research's existing funding directory. The useful addition is an explicit degree-stage filter, separate referee deadlines, funding-gap notes and unresolved-rule flags. Demand for this service has not yet been measured. [Existing Sciences Po service](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ecole-recherche/fr/carrieres/financements-appels-en-cours/) (P09-SR14).

## Findings and practical choices

### 1. Establish the institutional and applicant boundaries

CHEP is based at the Sciences Po Centre for History. Its public organisation page calls Jonathan Levy **Coordinator**, corroborated by the host page. The documented Cambridge/Harvard collaboration does not confer those universities' internal funding entitlements. The public account distinguishes collaboration beginning in 2019 from establishment in 2022; no anniversary interpretation is needed for this funding service. [Organisation](https://histecon.fr/en/organisation.html); [Sciences Po CHEP page](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/research/centre-for-history-and-economics-in-paris-chep/) (P09-SR02, SR04).

The corpus covers current students and researchers associated with Paris, including visitors and external affiliates where an external scheme permits them. It does not assume that all are enrolled or employed at Sciences Po. Five profiles guide selection: Master's; early doctorate; advanced doctorate; postdoctoral researcher; and other affiliate. “Advanced doctorate” is a research stage, not a universal year threshold: an ABD requirement must be translated carefully into a French programme. Nationality, residence, doctoral funding, society membership and ability to relocate remain unknown applicant facts.

All amounts are prospective awards or reimbursement limits. They are not CHEP income, actual expenditure or guaranteed personal entitlements. No total is calculated across currencies or award periods. A small US archival stipend may be useful within an already financed trip while leaving an unaffordable gap for a new transatlantic journey. The applicant should compare eligible costs, payment timing and remaining costs before drafting.

### 2. Strongest routes differ by profile

**Master's:** an existing paid RHS postgraduate member with a prepared historical project should examine **P09-O018** first. New membership is not a dependable shortcut to the December competition: the membership page gives a later closing date and an approximately eight-week decision process. For bilingual researchers, **P09-O009** offers subsidised methods exchange; Paris residents do not receive travel reimbursement. **P09-O003** applies to approved compulsory credited mobility, not a standalone archive visit. These are verified Master's-relevant options, but none establishes this user's personal eligibility. [RHS membership](https://royalhistsoc.org/membership/membership-postgraduate/); [CIERA workshop](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/24827); [Erasmus+](https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/fr/financer/bourses-aides-financieres/programme-erasmus-plus/) (P09-SS02, SF10, SF01).

The longer preparation pipeline includes CIERA Master's research mobility (**O005**), Hagley's two collection grants (**O013–014**) and EUI's Sørensen grant (**O016**). CIERA publishes an April 2027 deadline but has inconsistent residence/enrolment wording. Hagley's standing dates lack a dated next cycle, and Sørensen announces an early-2027 call without an exact deadline. Neither supplies a calendar date by extrapolation. BnF Hyman (**O017**) is retained as a closed, potentially useful M2 research example, not a promised renewal. [CIERA call](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/12189); [CIERA FAQ](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/node/12977); [Hagley overview](https://www.hagley.org/research/grants-fellowships?language=en); [Sørensen programme](https://www.eui.eu/en/academic-units/historical-archives-of-the-european-union/research-support/postgraduate-vibeke-soerensen-grant).

**Early doctorate:** **O001, CHEP**, is the first fit where institutional enrolment, year and topic match. It also explicitly covers language learning, a useful category absent from many archival awards. RHS postgraduate support is another modest research route for members. EHS poster participation (**O022**) has an imminent proposal gate, but any attendance contribution excludes travel; its bursary deadline and amount are unpublished. The Sciences Po exceptional doctoral fund (**O002**) is worth monitoring, including for researchers beyond CHEP's year limit. [CHEP call](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html); [EHS posters](https://ehs.org.uk/conference/new-researcher-posters/); [School doctoral funding](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ecole-recherche/fr/admissions/doctorat/doctorat-droits-scolarite-aides-financieres/) (P09-SR01, SS08, SR16).

**Advanced doctorate:** **O011, Newberry short-term**, is practical when the applicant meets ABD-equivalent conditions and can name essential collections. **O023, Harvard IRIS**, is a larger residential commitment and only suits international-relations/security research; it is external recruitment, not a CHEP exchange. **O021, EHS/IHR**, may suit a candidate who will submit the thesis by the specified fellowship-year threshold and can arrange British mentorship. The call and portal disagree on the January closing time, so a conservative earlier preparation target is recorded without pretending the conflict is resolved. [Newberry short-term](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/short-term-fellowships); [Harvard call](https://academicpositions.harvard.edu/postings/16700); [EHS call](https://ehs.org.uk/award/economic-history-society-fellowships/); [IHR portal](https://universityoflondon.smapply.io/prog/ihr_economic_history_society_fellowship_2027/).

**Postdoctoral and other affiliates:** RHS early-career support (**O019**) is proportionate for small research expenses where membership and PhD-age rules fit. Newberry long-term (**O012**) requires a completed doctorate and substantial residence; its near deadline makes it suitable mainly for an already developed proposal. CIERA young-doctor mobility (**O008**) offers a later planning horizon for those meeting its French degree/contract and residence conditions. Senior or independent scholars should check archive schemes individually; they should not select an early-career scheme solely because their CHEP affiliation is recent. [RHS ECR grants](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/early-career-research-funding/early-career-research-support-grants/); [Newberry long-term](https://www.newberry.org/research/fellowships/long-term-fellowships); [CIERA young doctors](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/12975).

### 3. Ranking and coverage gaps

The [profile shortlist](profile_shortlist.csv) ranks a **conditional order of checking**, not probability of winning. It applies five criteria in sequence: verified degree/affiliation eligibility; research relevance; usefulness for the actual expense; preparation burden; and deadline readiness. A missing hard eligibility fact overrides an attractive amount. No numerical prestige score is used. Burden estimates describe a prepared researcher's application effort, excluding research itself, relocation and event delivery.

Small collaborations have two documented routes: an open RHS workshop competition (**O020**) and a closed CIERA junior-conference call (**O010**). A Paris workshop venue and relevant RHS membership category still need confirmation. An eligible organiser, host support and a separate delivery budget would be necessary before proposing an event. The directory service itself should not absorb that workload. [RHS workshops](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/workshop-grants/rhs-workshop-grants/); [CIERA Colloques juniors](https://www.ciera.fr/fr/manifestation/24218).

The evidence does not establish a broad, unrestricted, immediately accessible Master's archive-travel pot at CHEP. Nor does it establish its absence internally. The coordinator and graduate administration could clarify unpublished support and reimbursement practices. One tempting route is positively excluded: Sciences Po says it will not participate in Eiffel's 2027 selection. EHS's general graduate research fund requires UK institutional study. Historical joint-centre grant forms and fellowship recipients cannot establish a new competition. [Eiffel](https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/en/fees-funding/bursaries-financial-aid/eiffel-scholarship/); [EHS graduate fund](https://ehs.org.uk/award/research-fund-for-graduate-students/); [historical Exchanges form](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/exel/exchanges_research_grant_application_2019.pdf).

### 4. Keep the service small and dependable

Each monthly review should open the call, guidance and application landing page; record the applicable cycle, access date and changes; then update the calendar and profile verdict together. Give near deadlines a short weekly check. Use five visible status labels matching the CSV. If a deadline passes, close that cycle; if a new date is absent, move the scheme to the watchlist. If a page cannot be retrieved, preserve the prior record and mark current availability unverified. Never silently roll a date into another year.

An entry untouched for 30 days should display **“recheck required”** and leave the featured digest until checked. That is a service rule, not a funder expiry. Corrections can use a simple proposed form: opportunity ID, disputed field, official URL, suggested correction and optional reply address. The proposed research-support owner reviews changes; maintain a dated change note. Collect no citizenship, income or personal application documents in this public directory. Users assess those facts privately.

After six weeks, continue only if someone owns maintenance and trial readers can identify an appropriate route without mistaking conditional eligibility for approval. If maintenance exceeds the estimate, retain a shorter featured subset while preserving the evidence archive. The 24-entry directory is a checked starting corpus, not an obligation to maintain every worldwide scheme.

## Methods, decisions and limits

This run read `prompts_proposal.md` and the non-empty `Summary.md`, applied the shared brief and executed P09 only. Three parallel research components examined French/European, archival/library and society/partner routes. Official calls and guidance supplied terms; historical or ambiguous pages were retained as explicit watchlist evidence or excluded. No other P01–P12 report was required or awaited.

The [coverage note](coverage.md) records source branches, failures, outstanding leads and substantive conflicts. Web results include indexed/cached representations; access date is not publication date, and retrieved text does not prove a portal transaction will work. The source register preserves these distinctions. No accounts were created and no funder was contacted. The two [preparation checklists](applicant_guide.md) and [deadline calendar](deadline_calendar.md) make the research actionable while leaving applicant-specific facts and institutional decisions explicit.
