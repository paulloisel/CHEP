## Strongest findings and decisions

- **A current Master's-eligible route exists, with an important readiness gate.** The [RHS Postgraduate Research Support Grant](https://royalhistsoc.org/research_funding/postgraduate-research-funding/pgr-research-support-grants/) includes research Master's and PhD students in History. It closes on **4 December 2026**. Existing paid membership matters: the [membership page](https://royalhistsoc.org/membership/membership-postgraduate/) has its next membership deadline on 14 December and reports an approximately eight-week decision period. An unaffiliated prospective applicant cannot assume joining now permits this grant round. The call normally expects sufficiently developed research, often years 2–4. The referee must upload before the applicant can submit. Sources SS01–03, accessed 2026-11-15.
- **Two distinct uses for doctoral/postdoctoral affiliates:** the RHS early-career award supports individual research, while the workshop grant supports a one-day collaborative activity. Paris workshop location is not explicitly confirmed; retain this as a condition rather than asserting funding for a Paris event. RHS international guidance says other programmes remain available to international members under their individual terms. Sources SS03–05.
- **The EHS/IHR fellowship is genuinely open to applicants who need to arrange a British mentor later.** The [public application portal](https://universityoflondon.smapply.io/prog/ihr_economic_history_society_fellowship_2027/) opened on 15 October. Both pages give **31 January 2027**, but EHS says **11:00 GMT** and the portal **11:59 AM GMT**. Keep the discrepancy visible and plan completion before the earlier time. Sources SS06–07.
- **Conference funding is partial.** The EHS poster-proposal deadline is now **18 November 2026 (extended)**. The Society's bursary contribution explicitly excludes travel. The proposal deadline is not a separately verified bursary deadline; don't relabel it. Sources SS08.
- **EHA future dates do not establish an open application.** The early-stage, graduate-dissertation, and Cole scheme pages publish January/March 2027 deadlines, while their linked application pages say closed. Retain only the early-stage row as `unverified` / evidence `conflicting`; the other two are optional watchlist leads. Sources SS10–11, SS21–24.
- **Harvard affiliation is not an eligibility shortcut.** The IRIS predoctoral fellowship is an external, theme-specific recruitment opportunity for advanced doctoral work on international relations/security; it is not a CHEP entitlement or a documented centre exchange. Its applicant and reference deadlines differ: **15 and 22 January 2027**. Source SS09.

## Exclusions, unresolved leads, and next retrievals

**Exclude from a Paris-only applicant's list:** [EHS graduate research funding](https://ehs.org.uk/award/research-fund-for-graduate-students/) explicitly requires enrolment at a UK university or college. A separate UK enrolment could change an individual's eligibility; it is not assumed. This is a particularly tempting false Master's match. SS12.

**Exclude historical exchanges from the current calendar:** the [Cambridge-Harvard Exchanges grant form](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/exel/exchanges_research_grant_application_2019.pdf) explicitly covers **2015–2021**. Its recurring June/December dates must not become a 2026 deadline. It does not establish a present grant route for CHEP students. SS15.

**EHP remains a verification queue, not a ready application.** The checked [programme description](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/ehppf/about.html) describes nomination and selection, while the [CHEP-EHP page](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/ehppf/chep_fellows.html) identifies a 2021 cohort. None of the checked pages gives a current cycle, amount, application instructions, or deadline. A centre administrator would need to clarify whether nominations are currently invited before it is promoted as a service. SS16–18.

**WIGH cycle conflict unresolved.** The [funding page](https://wigh.wcfia.harvard.edu/funding) announces an October 2026 opening for 2027–28 but retains 2025–26 eligibility and December 2024 deadlines. The linked application redirects to a generic vacancy index. Do not roll the dates forward or present the older stipend as a current offer. Next step is a fresh official vacancy search; current terms were not verified. SS19–20.

**Optional EHA leads:** [Graduate Dissertation Fellowships](https://eh.net/graduate-dissertation-fellowships/) list 14 January 2027, support for 2027–28, one application for awards of $10,000 or $17,500, and two letters. [Arthur H. Cole Grants](https://eh.net/arthur-h-cole-grants/) list 1 March 2027, normally up to $5,000 for PhD-holding EHA members, with a three-page proposal/budget and CV. Both linked submission pages say closed. These would merit rechecking when EHA updates its forms; neither is ready to apply in this pass. SS21–24.

**RHS International Research Support** is closed for 2026–27, with the next call unannounced. It is for international Fellows/Associate Fellows resident outside the UK/Ireland and excludes postgraduate members. Retain in a longer-term affiliate watchlist if useful, but do not use it to substantiate Master's eligibility. SS03.

## Practical interpretation

The highest immediate student service value is an eligibility screen, not a large prestige-based list: degree classification, membership status, research purpose, actual trip costs, and referee readiness often eliminate a seemingly attractive application. An open call is not synonymous with an individual ready to apply. EHS conference attendance aid should include an explicit uncovered-travel warning. A workshop proposal should have an academic lead and confirmed venue eligibility before student preparation grows beyond a short concept note.

Suggested next checks, not actions taken: resolve EHS deadline-time conflict; clarify RHS Paris workshop location and membership category; recheck EHA submission availability; identify a current WIGH vacancy; ask an institutional counterpart about EHP nominations; retry Cambridge through its official navigation. No funds, institutional support, or applicant eligibility have been assumed.
