## Crosswalk scope and mechanics

[entity_crosswalk.csv](entity_crosswalk.csv) contains **726 mappings**: 111 person-source rows (72 P01 identities plus all 39 P02 cohort rows), 33 P10 person-role references, 22 organisations, 126 P01 role anchors, 231 publication work rows, one publication container, one supplementary contribution link, 173 event-source rows and 28 milestones. People retain P01-based P13-P numbering; Lina Skoglund adds P13-P073 using her author page and P10's institutional role evidence. Original IDs are never overwritten. P13-R numbers anchor P01 role rows; missing current committee roles should be added by the root overlay from the verified page, not retroactively written into P01.

Works use P13-W numbering inherited from P02, with new P11 concepts P13-W201–213 and the additional Parrinello chapter P13-W214. The *Tracés* issue container is P13-C001. Underlying works are linked through manually inspected title, author, DOI/ISBN, canonical URLs and original translation notes. Edition IDs remain distinct whenever identifiers differ or do not suffice to prove an exact manifestation match. Thus hardcover/ebook or paperback/original date differences never manufacture new research works.

The three bibliographic tables have **232 rows** (188 P02 + 36 P11 + 8 P10). They map to **197 work concepts plus one issue container**. One of the 197 is Balguy's unidentified contribution to *Lexique des réparations de l’esclavage*: P02 correctly parks it in the supplementary table; P11 includes a volume-level record without identifying an entry. Excluding it gives **196 provisional identified published-text work concepts**. This is a mechanical union inheriting source uncertainties, not a new verified publication census; do not use it in the meeting headline. P02 remains 183 provisional work IDs; P11 remains 31 work IDs in its original inventory; P10's strict annual indicator remains two dated contributions. No claim of completeness follows from these numbers.

P11 adds 13 concepts beyond P02: one reception article, two chapters, the unresolved Lexique contribution, five Chaker texts and four Giosuè texts. Excluding Lexique leaves 12. P10 adds the Parrinello chapter and a separately excluded *Tracés* issue container. Six other P10 work records match P02. Exact first-release boundaries remain unresolved for Génard's September 2025 book announcement; the year and work existence are supported without a precise publication day.

All **22 P10 event rows** match existing P04 events by exact date and manually compared title. P10's 14 core/shared listings comprise four held events and ten with unverified delivery; eight wider-network sessions are contextual. [Paris's retrospective](https://histecon.fr/en/events_recent.html) establishes that the 26 June 2026 workshop was held, overriding an older future-tense announcement but not proving attendance or spending. The 26 June 2025 predecessor remains distinct. The *Invisible Actors* [detailed schedule](https://www.sciencespo.fr/recherche/en/events/conference-junior-invisible-actors-in-the-making-of-international-law/) supports 27–28 November 2025 over the index's 26–27 dates, while delivery remains unknown. ## Limits and integration

[entity_conflicts.csv](entity_conflicts.csv) contains 16 reconciliation decisions, including unresolved alternatives and practical effects. Identity matching does not reverify every role, bibliography record or event; original evidence statuses remain visible. The association roster establishes listed roles, not automatically current employers. Skoglund's dated visit is retained as visiting researcher because the host's postdoctoral label begins before the author-reported PhD date. Paris, Cambridge, Harvard, their Joint Center and the shared EHC programme retain distinct organisational IDs. A partnership link never becomes a reporting line.

Prioritise role title, committee correction, selected-publication denominator and anniversary basis in the meeting; retain the more detailed date and manifestation issues for an editor's internal check sheet. No contact, publication or institutional approval occurred.
