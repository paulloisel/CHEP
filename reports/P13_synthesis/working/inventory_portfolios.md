## Evidence precedence and gaps affecting choice

P05 inspected live bilingual pages, the mailing-list form and executive committee where several other reports obtained failed or cached retrievals. Its dated observation is better evidence of the displayed surface at inspection time; upstream failure remains an honest retrieval limitation, not evidence of absent pages or an inactive service. P05 measured a 1,402-pixel homepage width at a 390-pixel viewport, with a screenshot and inspection log. This is a direct service defect. It does not prove every page or every device fails.

P06–P08 and P12 give good thematic and format proposals, but do not establish unmet demand for a new digest, speaker series, symposium or peer group. Current host programmes already welcome students and researchers. The validated problem is more limited: multiple entry routes, precise eligibility distinctions, source/role/date inconsistencies and an observed mobile layout obstacle. The possible benefit of a consolidated entry guide remains a hypothesis suitable for a small task test.

P03 cannot establish an available discretionary budget. Its €0.9m accounting observation must not become a grant or spending total. P09's call ceilings cannot become received funds. P10's annual prototype and P11's retrospective differ in period and editorial purpose; they should not be treated as concurrent default commitments.

## Three mutually exclusive portfolios under a common 90-day horizon

Use **twelve active seven-day planning weeks =60 student hours**, then days85–90 as an unallocated closure window, not an invented extra five-hour allowance. The five-hour cap applies to every seven-day week; averages do not excuse peaks. Existing duties must be counted within that cap. Estimates include student coordination as stated; institutional support remains separate.

### A. Low-effort service improvement — recommended

Three actions: (1) reviewed entry page; (2) small shared fact register and eligibility-first call card; (3) task-test, handover and a technical acceptance specification. Reuse P05/P12/P09 prototypes; do not launch a general funding advice service or a second newsletter.

**20h setup +14h recurring maintenance/coordination (1.75h×8 weeks) +3h evaluation/handover =37h**, leaving23h unallocated within60h. Weeks1–4 use5h each; weeks5–12 use1.75h. Add0.5h evaluation in week6 and2.5h handover/evaluation in week12: peak5h and final active week4.25h. Routine counterpart coordination is inside the20h and1.75h lines, not an uncounted extra. Days85–90 add no planned work.

Separate support: use P05's **4–8h editor/admin review**, plus technical repair only if accepted:8–14 developer and2–4 accessibility hours. The37h student plan can finish as a reviewable internal handover without a technical implementation commitment. Any urgent correction displaces another task; the23h margin is not permission to add a project. Check the entry guide with3–5 consenting users only after institutional permission for outreach; draft the test now.

### B. Community-building pilot — alternative

Adopt P12's one welcome guide, three60-minute sessions and bounded optional follow-up as one programme: **14h setup+38h delivery/maintenance/coordination/evaluation=52h over12 weeks**, peak≤5h according to its week table, leaving8h contingency. Separate8 academic+3 admin/editor hours. No standing series or simultaneous digest, annual report or full website package. Cash baseline conditional€0; optional refreshments€36 can be omitted.

Use the existing P12 schedule rather than inventing an even average: its first support/overlap gate is week2 and interest gate week3. If no sponsor or no demonstrable need, retain the guide and stop session preparation. This is better when Levy prioritises exchange and can provide academic support; current public evidence alone does not establish that priority.

### C. Research-visibility package — alternative

Choose P10's modest annual edition: (1) evidence register/scope; (2) one six-page edition; (3) archive/correction handover. **8h setup+32h first-cycle repeatable work=40h**. The32h include6h record recovery,24h drafting/contributor coordination/review corrections and2h archive preparation. Its workflow uses2 setup weeks×4h,2 recovery weeks×3h and6 production weeks×4h, with the two archive hours in production weeks5–6 to bring those weeks to5h. Thus ten scheduled weeks total40h, never over5h;20h remain unallocated within the common60h ceiling. Routine annual capture after release is0.5h/month; 32h annual work is not32h every12 weeks.

Separate~10h institutional support plus10–15minutes per contributor. Conditional€0 text-only web edition, with PDF/full translation/printing deferred. Missing facts are qualified or removed, not filled. The25h P11 retrospective could replace this edition if the institutional priority is a2027 establishment retrospective; it must not be added automatically. The anniversary basis still needs strategic choice even if its two dates are established.
