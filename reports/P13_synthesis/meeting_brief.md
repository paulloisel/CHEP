# CHEP: make the next step easier

**For discussion with Jonathan Levy, Coordinator · 15 November 2026 · Draft**

**Recommendation:** choose a small service improvement for the first 90 days. Use the existing research and prototypes to help people find activities, understand eligibility and follow research links. Decide whether it fits CHEP before committing to a new programme.

## Five findings worth acting on

1. **There is already an entry point into scholarly exchange.** *Histoires de la vie économique* welcomes students and researchers from inside and outside Sciences Po; its 2026–27 programme credits CHEP support. This supports clearer signposting. Demand for an additional group remains untested. [Official programme](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/) (P13-SS001).
2. **The website has a demonstrated access problem, alongside current content.** P05 measured a 1,402-pixel homepage at a 390-pixel viewport. P13 live checks found current French and English notices and a registration embed. Prior retrieval failures do not establish a broken site or absent mailing list. [Mobile evidence](../P05_website/evidence/home-en-mobile-390.jpg), [inspection record](../P05_website/evidence/inspection_notes.md), [live homepage](https://histecon.fr/en/index.html) (P13-LIVE02).
3. **Funding guidance must begin with eligibility.** The current CHEP call covers Sciences Po PhD years 1–3, with access to up to €1,000 of 2027 research expenses. Applications close **11 December 2026**; references **15 December**. It is not a Master's route or an expenditure total. [Official call](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html), live rechecked (P13-LIVE04).
4. **Publication visibility can improve without inflating output.** The checked public selection has **18 displayed editions representing 14 works**. P02's wider catalogue remains partial; neither count measures CHEP-funded production. Use sourced reading cards with affiliation and edition labels. [Public selection](https://histecon.fr/en/publications.html), [reconciled evidence](working/entities_review.md).
5. **The anniversary needs an explicit basis.** A launch is documented on **5 June 2019**; current accounts distinguish establishment in **2022**. A fifth establishment anniversary falls in **2027**; its exact day is unverified. Paris, Cambridge and Harvard retain distinct institutional identities. [Launch account](https://histecon.fr/en/spatial.html), [institutional account](https://histecon.fr/en/index.html).

## Resources and alternatives

**37 student hours:** four setup weeks at 5 h, then eight weeks at 1.75 h upkeep/coordination, plus 3 h evaluation. Peak: 5 h/week. This leaves **23 h unallocated** within a conservative 60 h envelope; days 85–90 add no assumed hours. Estimate **6 institutional review hours** separately. Incremental cash is **€0 only if existing tools and staff are supplied**. A technical repair would additionally need 8–14 developer and 2–4 accessibility-review hours, or a separately agreed budget; it is not promised by this portfolio.

| Alternative, replacing this portfolio | Student requirement | Why defer now? |
|---|---|---|
| Community pilot: guide, three peer sessions, bounded follow-up | 14 h setup + 38 h delivery/upkeep = **52 h/12 weeks**; 11 institutional h | Interest, overlap, convenor and room/platform unconfirmed |
| Research visibility: digital reading edition and two digest issues | 33 h setup + 19.5 h production/upkeep = **52.5 h/12 weeks**; 7.5 institutional h | Editorial demand and distribution ownership unconfirmed |

These are alternatives, not a menu to accumulate. Defer the full annual report, speaker series, symposium, printed retrospective and full funding-directory maintenance. See the [portfolio comparison](portfolio_options.md) and [90-day plan](90_day_plan.md).

## Three decisions for this meeting

1. **Strategic fit:** choose the service portfolio, one alternative, or no pilot; accept a five-hour weekly ceiling and the stated deferrals.
2. **Scope and counterpart:** identify functions willing to review a 12-record sample and entry guide; confirm the six-hour institutional contribution and an existing publication route, or retain an internal draft.
3. **Continuation rule:** accept reviews at weeks 4, 6 and 12. Continue only with a maintenance owner, reliable sources, and successful task checks; narrow the service if upkeep exceeds two hours in two successive weeks.

Success means three consenting trial readers can locate an appropriate activity and correctly distinguish grant eligibility and deadlines, with no critical error after one revision. Approval of fit does **not** authorise publication, spending or contacting people; those permissions follow once the exact action is reviewable.
