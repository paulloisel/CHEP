# CHEP: make the next step easier

**For discussion with Jonathan Levy, Coordinator · 15 November 2026 · Draft**

**Recommendation:** choose a small service improvement for 90 days: help people find activities, understand eligibility and follow research links. Reuse the existing prototypes.

## Five findings worth acting on

1. **An entry point already exists.** *Histoires de la vie économique* welcomes students and researchers from inside and outside Sciences Po, with CHEP support. Clearer signposting is useful; demand for another group is untested. [2026–27 programme](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/) (P13-SS001).
2. **Mobile access needs repair.** P05 measured a 1,402-pixel homepage at a 390-pixel viewport. P13 live checks found current bilingual notices and a registration embed; failed retrievals did not establish an absent service. [Mobile evidence](../P05_website/evidence/home-en-mobile-390.jpg), [inspection record](../P05_website/evidence/inspection_notes.md), [live homepage](https://histecon.fr/en/index.html).
3. **Funding starts with eligibility.** CHEP's call covers Sciences Po PhD years 1–3, up to €1,000 of 2027 research expenses. Applications close **11 December 2026**; references **15 December**. It is not a Master's route or expenditure total. [Live-rechecked terms](https://histecon.fr/en/research_grants.html) (P13-LIVE04).
4. **Editions are not unique outputs.** The public selection has **18 editions representing 14 works**. The wider catalogue remains partial; neither count measures CHEP-funded production. [Public selection](https://histecon.fr/en/publications.html), [reconciliation](working/entities_review.md).
5. **Name the anniversary basis.** A **5 June 2019 launch** and **2022 establishment** are documented. The fifth establishment anniversary is **2027**; its exact day remains unverified. Keep Paris and wider-network attribution separate. [Launch](https://histecon.fr/en/spatial.html), [institutional account](https://histecon.fr/en/index.html).

## Resources and alternatives

**37 student hours:** four weeks at 5 h, eight at 1.75 h upkeep/coordination, plus 3 h evaluation. Peak: 5 h/week; **23 h remain unallocated** within 60 h. Days 85–90 add no assumed hours. Allow **6 institutional review hours** separately. Cash is **€0 only with existing tools/staff**. Technical repair additionally needs 8–14 developer and 2–4 accessibility hours, or a separately agreed budget; implementation is uncommitted.

| Alternative, replacing this portfolio | Student requirement | Why defer now? |
|---|---|---|
| Community pilot: guide, three peer sessions, bounded follow-up | 14 h setup + 38 h delivery/upkeep = **52 h/12 weeks**; 11 institutional h | Interest, overlap, convenor and room/platform unconfirmed |
| Research visibility: digital reading edition and two digest issues | 33 h setup + 19.5 h production/upkeep = **52.5 h/12 weeks**; 7.5 institutional h | Editorial demand and distribution ownership unconfirmed |

Defer the annual report, speaker series, symposium, printing and full funding-directory maintenance. See [portfolio comparison](portfolio_options.md) and [90-day plan](90_day_plan.md).

## Three decisions for this meeting

1. **Strategic fit:** choose service, one alternative, or no pilot; accept the five-hour ceiling and deferrals.
2. **Scope/counterpart:** identify reviewers for the guide/12-record sample; confirm six institutional hours and an existing publication route, or retain an internal draft.
3. **Continuation:** agree reviews at weeks 4, 6 and 12. Require an owner, reliable sources and successful task checks; narrow scope after two successive upkeep weeks above two hours.

Success: three consenting readers locate an appropriate activity and correctly identify eligibility/deadlines after at most one revision. Strategic fit does **not** authorise publication, spending or contacting people; seek those permissions for specific later actions. Nothing has been sent or scheduled.
