“The research gives us a practical starting point. CHEP already supports a reading workshop open to students and researchers, and its website carries current notices. I propose one small service package: an entry guide, a shared fact register, and testing and handover with a technical-fix brief. It would take about 37 student hours over twelve weeks, including upkeep, with separate institutional review. I can bring a first review packet within five hours. Today I would like to settle whether that contribution fits, which functions could review it, and what evidence should determine whether we continue.
