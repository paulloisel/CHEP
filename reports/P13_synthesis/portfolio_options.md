# Three alternatives within five student hours a week

**As of 2026-11-15. Estimates, not confirmed capacity or supplier quotations.** Choose one portfolio. The planning period is twelve working weeks (60 hours), with days 85–90 reserved for elapsed-time slippage and no extra allocation assumed. Start is relative to an agreed brief, not 15 November. Holidays, examinations and approval delays may extend elapsed time; reduce scope rather than recover lost time through overtime.

## A — Service improvement, recommended

Reuse [P05's content package](../P05_website/report.md), [P09's eligibility notice](../P09_opportunities/report.md#draft-bilingual-notice--120-words-total) and [P12's welcome draft](../P12_community/pilot_plan.md#example-welcome-page--draft-for-review). Three actions: entry guide; small fact register; task testing/handover and technical specification. The register covers no more than 12 selected people, works/editions, events and calls. It is not a commitment to maintain all research inventories.

Setup: **20 hours**, including initial coordination and checks. Weeks 5–12: **1.75 hours weekly** (0.5 events/access; 0.25 call checks; 0.5 records/language; 0.25 routine coordination; 0.25 averaged monthly reconciliation). Evaluation/handover: **3 additional hours**. Total **37**, mean **3.08/week**, maximum **5**, unallocated **23**. A1 totals 10 hours; A2 18; A3 9. The maintenance allowance and action totals are two views of the same time.

Institutional review: **6 hours**, estimated as 2 academic, 2 communications/web-editor and 2 combined event/funding/bilingual input. These may be shared functions, not three appointments. Optional technical implementation: **8–14 developer + 2–4 accessibility hours**, excluded from the student's 37 and the 6 review hours. P05's outside-labour illustration is €600–€1,800 at assumed rates, not a quote; no budget is confirmed for it. Produce a specification even if repair cannot be staffed. Drafting cash is conditionally €0 using existing tools. A technical defect cannot be declared fixed from copy changes.

Validated need: mobile overflow and dispersed, differently scoped records. Proposed benefit: easier access and fewer errors. Newcomer demand and improved task performance require testing. High reversibility: keep drafts internally, replace a short page, or stop updates with a dated archive. Main dependency: a responsible editor, not a new platform.

## B — Community-building pilot

Adopt the bounded [P12 pilot](../P12_community/pilot_plan.md) as the main project: welcome guide, three 60-minute peer sessions, voluntary follow-up. **14 setup + 38 delivery/maintenance/coordination/evaluation = 52 hours**, mean **4.33/week**, maximum **5**. Institutional support: **8 academic + 3 administrative/editor hours**. Conditional €0 baseline requires a free approved room/platform, access support and convenor. Optional €36 refreshments are excluded and unapproved. Participants' voluntary preparation remains separate.

The existing programme establishes a scholarly setting, not unmet demand. Before starting sessions, require a sponsor, overlap check, four interested participants and one willing contributor. If these are absent, stop sessions and hand over only the guide. Later continuation requires the P12 usefulness and repeat-participation measures, with response denominators. No permanent mentoring or international travel. Reversibility is moderate: cancel future sessions before invitations/booking commitments, and avoid creating an indefinite series.

Only **8 hours** remain within 60. P05's full 20-hour refresh cannot be added: 52 + 20 = 72, even before its later upkeep. Some guide work overlaps, but no unmeasured saving is credited. Defer the refresh, newsletter and funding-directory service; include only event information essential to this pilot.

## C — Research-visibility package

Use [P11's text-and-link digital retrospective](../P11_retrospective/retrospective_draft.md) and [P06's digest prototype](../P06_communications/pilot_newsletter.md). Produce one reviewed reading edition and **two**, not three, digest issues six weeks apart. P06's original three-issue plan spans 18 weeks; it has not been compressed into 90 days. Anniversary branding requires agreement on the 2022 basis; a non-anniversary reading guide is the fallback.

Setup: **25 hours** for milestone/scope and reading edition, plus **8** for digest/template/archive setup = **33**. Recurring/delivery: **13** for two issues, **3** for 12 weekly checks, **1.5** for three monthly edition reviews, **2** for evaluation = **19.5**. Total **52.5**, mean **4.375/week**, maximum **5**, unallocated **7.5**. No assumed efficiency saving from shared records. Institutional input: P11 academic/web **4 hours** plus P06 setup **1.5** and two issue reviews/distributions **2** = **7.5 hours**, plus any unusually extensive contributor corrections. Conditional €0 with existing publishing tools. No covers, printing or fully translated second edition.

There is verified material to interpret; audience demand and duplication of existing communications are hypotheses. Requires academic editor, bilingual check, list administrator and archive route. If distribution overlaps an existing host newsletter, substitute a CHEP block only after agreeing an equivalent smaller scope. Reversibility is moderate: drafts can stop easily; published issues need stable archiving/correction. Defer the 40-hour P10 annual-report cycle, speaker programme and public event. Adding P10 would make **92.5 hours**, exceeding 60 by **32.5**, before any additional demands. If one issue slips, publish only one after approval; do not take hours from another week already at five.

## Basis for choosing

No numerical scores or weights are used. Priority order: evidence of a practical need; ability to finish with confirmed functions; useful scholarly/community benefit; sustainable maintenance; cash and dependency exposure; reversibility. These are judgement criteria, not estimated effects. A wins because it addresses an observed defect and creates reusable, bounded service material while leaving room for the student's research and unexpected corrections. B could become preferable if a convenor confirms unmet feedback needs. C could become preferable if CHEP already wants a 2027 publication and has reliable editorial capacity. The [proposal table](proposal_comparison.csv) keeps those choices and the larger deferred projects visible.
