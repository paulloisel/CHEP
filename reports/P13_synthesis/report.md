**as_of_date: 2026-11-15 (America/Los_Angeles). Research and meeting draft.** All twelve workstreams are present. ## Decision brief

Recommend **portfolio A: an entry guide, a small shared fact register, and testing/handover with a mobile-fix specification**. Reuse the substantive prototypes rather than restart their research. The student can deliver a first review packet within five hours, then complete a **37-hour, twelve-week programme**, including maintenance and coordination. Each week stays within five hours. Six institutional review hours are estimated separately; actual availability is unconfirmed. Cash is conditionally €0 with existing tools and staff. Technical repair needs additional expertise and cannot be promised by this student allocation.

The strongest need evidence is P05's measured mobile overflow, plus source discrepancies that can mislead readers about roles, dates and eligibility. Existing provision includes a [CHEP-supported reading workshop open to students and researchers](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/). Public sources establish an opportunity to improve access; they do not establish demand for another event series or subscription. The recommended service is small enough to test and withdraw if it duplicates existing arrangements.

Two alternatives remain useful: **52 hours** for P12's welcome/three-session/follow-up community pilot, or **52.5 hours** for a digital reading edition and two digest issues. They replace A. A full annual report, symposium, external-speaker programme, printed retrospective and global funding-directory service are explicitly deferred. All three portfolios distinguish student effort, institutional support, maintenance and conditional cash in [portfolio_options.md](portfolio_options.md).

The meeting should settle three things: strategic fit and scope; reviewing/maintenance functions and actual resources; and the first deliverable plus continuation criteria. Later permission to publish, spend or contact people remains separate. Start with the [meeting brief](meeting_brief.md) and [twenty-minute agenda](meeting_agenda.md), keeping the evidence tables available for questions.

## Method, identifiers and evidence strength

The workflow compared each report's scope and units before combining records. People were anchored to P01 identities and professional URLs, with dated roles separate. Works were matched using authors, titles, DOI/ISBN, canonical URLs and edition notes. Events used dates and manually compared titles, retaining role and delivery status. Funding retained cycle, legal party, recipient, parent/components, currency and amount basis. Internal P13 IDs are cross-references, not invented ORCIDs, DOIs or grant numbers.

[id_crosswalk.csv](id_crosswalk.csv) preserves original file/record IDs, canonical identity, edition IDs, evidence, match basis and notes. It contains 2,086 mappings, including every original master-source row; it is an overlay, not a replacement database or a merge of every unconnected record. Source-identity groups normalise fragments and only inspected CHEP home/www aliases. Language variants remain separate observations even where a content chain is demonstrably shared. The 1,254 input source rows reduce to **718 URL identity groups**, still not 718 independent witnesses: translations and cross-posts can share underlying evidence. Original URLs and retrieval conditions remain available. All original P01–P12 files were left unchanged.

[reconciliation_log.csv](reconciliation_log.csv) records 31 decisions or retained uncertainties, with affected outputs and practical consequences. `verified` means supported for the stated fact and scope, not exhaustive or live in every case; `inferred` marks analytic joins/planning judgements; `conflicting` retains incompatible alternatives; `unverified` prevents absent evidence becoming zero. Synthesis [sources.csv](sources.csv) has 54 primary-observation/input records, including 40 web or preserved-primary observations and 14 local instruction/report references. Cache ages, failed routes and live inspection are distinguished. Repeated reports never substitute for inspection of the responsible source.

Proposal comparison is qualitative. It prioritises demonstrated need, deliverability, scholarly/community benefit, sustainable maintenance, cash/dependencies and reversibility. No numerical score implies an estimated impact. All workload figures are planning estimates. The [comparison CSV](proposal_comparison.csv) contains aggregate portfolios and underlying deferred projects; rows must not be summed. `setup_hours` refers to student time; `recurring_hours` states its period explicitly. Staff effort appears in dependencies. Unknown cost is not zero, and conditional €0 excludes the value of supplied labour. The [weekly schedules](portfolio_options.md#weekly-capacity-check) verify peak capacity, not just averages.
