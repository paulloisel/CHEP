# A 90-day service pilot

**Draft as of 2026-11-15. Recommended portfolio A.** Day 1 is the start agreed after a strategic-fit discussion, not an assumed calendar booking. The scenario provides five student hours in each of twelve working weeks, conservatively 60 hours; days 85–90 carry no additional allocation. No outreach, publication or spending is authorised by this plan.

## Three actions and first deliverable

**A1 — Entry guide (6 setup + 4 later hours).** A short English/French guide links authoritative activities and separates attending, receiving notices, formal affiliation and funding. Reuse [P12's welcome draft](../P12_community/pilot_plan.md#example-welcome-page--draft-for-review) and the [P05 entry/event prototypes](../P05_website/page_prototypes.md). The event/access editor owns access facts; the student prepares copy.

**A2 — Shared fact register (10 setup + 8 later hours).** Limit the first service to 12 selected records across people/roles, works/editions, events and calls. Keep canonical URL, stable local ID, source date/access date, relationship, evidence status, owner, next review and expiry. Retain unresolved alternatives in the reconciliation log. The academic editor owns attribution; the research-support administrator owns call wording. A selected-record service cannot produce a membership, publication or expenditure census.

**A3 — Test, hand over and specify the technical fix (4 setup + 5 later hours).** Use short task checks, written expiry rules and a mobile-layout issue specification. The communications/web owner decides the route and maintenance allocation. Layout implementation requires separate technical capacity. Do not report it as fixed until it has actually been retested.

**Within the first five student hours:** spend 2 hours on scope, source/role corrections and counterpart questions; 2 on the annotated guide; 1 on the starter register. Output: a single review packet, linked to the existing prototypes, with no new mailing list or copied calendar.

No scheduled week exceeds five hours. Weeks 5–12 reserve 1.75 hours each; week 6 adds 0.5 and week 12 adds 2.5. Routine coordination and handover are included, not hidden in spare time. Maintenance allocation: 0.5 events/access, 0.25 call checks, 0.5 records/language, 0.25 coordination, 0.25 averaged monthly reconciliation. Track actual time against these categories. The six institutional hours comprise 2 academic, 2 communications/web-editor and 2 combined event/funding/bilingual input; availability is unknown.

## Dependencies and decision rules

**Strategic-fit gate, before week 1:** agree that a small access/information service is useful and name counterpart functions. This is a decision about work, not permission to publish, approach participants or spend. If no counterpart accepts responsibility by the end of week 2, cap work at the first 10 hours and hand over internal drafts. Do not continue maintenance without an owner.

**Accuracy gate, week 4:** every featured record must have a source, date and role/eligibility/status label; unresolved facts are qualified or removed. The current CHEP call is for PhD years 1–3, with application/reference dates 11/15 December 2026; recheck at the actual implementation date. If the cycle has closed, archive it or link the responsible general service; do not roll its dates forward. A past event announcement is not proof of delivery. Funding ceilings are not actual awards or expenditure.

**Usefulness gate, weeks 6 and 12:** after separate permission to approach them, three consenting readers should each locate an activity appropriate to their situation and explain the relevant eligibility and deadline distinction. Record task denominators, obstacles and critical errors, without collecting personal funding applications. Proposed success: all three complete these two tasks after at most one revision, no critical factual error, and a maintenance owner remains. Three trials are a small usability check, not evidence of general community demand. If recruitment is unavailable, report user evidence as missing and retain an internal prototype.

**Capacity/stop rule:** if upkeep exceeds two hours in two successive weeks, reduce the featured records or link directly to the authoritative service. Do not spend the 23-hour margin on an additional initiative by default. If sources or approvals cannot be kept reliable, withdraw time-sensitive copy and keep a dated archive. If two rounds of task checks still reveal critical errors, stop public rollout and simplify. Continue after day 90 only with recorded ownership, affordable actual hours and an explicit scope decision.

## Cash and technical work

Drafting has a conditional €0 incremental cash baseline using existing institutional tools and staff; this is not a valuation of labour at zero. No confirmed discretionary budget exists. P05 estimates **8–14 developer + 2–4 accessibility-review hours** for repair, separate from this plan's hours and uncommitted. If those are unavailable, deliver the technical brief and retain the measured defect as open. An external repair would need a scoped quote and later spending authority. Do not replace that dependency with a student-led rebuild.

## Explicitly deferred

Defer the P06 standalone digest, P07 external-speaker series, P08 symposium, P09 full 24-entry service, P10 annual edition and P11 printed/anniversary release. P12's three-session pilot is an alternative portfolio, not an add-on. Also defer exhaustive bibliographic completion, private finance reconstruction, subscriber analytics, full translation of all materials and a website redesign. Their prototypes remain useful evidence; deferral does not reject their scholarly value.

At week 12, one of the alternatives in [portfolio_options.md](portfolio_options.md) may replace the service pilot if evidence and counterparts justify it. Existing service maintenance must be transferred, narrowed or included in the new five-hour budget before that choice is made.
