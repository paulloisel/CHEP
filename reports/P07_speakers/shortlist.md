## P07-C014 — Elizabeth Chatterjee

**Documented position:** Assistant Professor of Environmental History and the College; DPhil 2015, University of Chicago.

Chatterjee compares energy protests by US truckers and North Indian farmers. Her article examines how energy-dependent small owners organise demands on the state, connecting class formation to carbon-intensive livelihoods.

**Proposed question:** When does a claim to cheap energy become class politics—and where does the comparison break down?

**Audience:** Environmental, South Asian, social and economic historians; students practising comparative argument.

**Proposed respondent(s):** Giacomo Parrinello: environmental infrastructure and state/capital; Jonathan Levy: climate/capitalism; inferred.

**Prior record:** verified scheduled: La matière du politique, 2022-04-08, Fuel Rioters; host-series precedent, not proof of a separate CHEP invitation or completed event.

**Evidence and practical limits:** Open article abstract, opening and conclusion inspected; not a close reading of all sections. Related 2022 host-series talk makes this a proposed published-paper follow-up, conditional on convenor judgment. Online timing, fees and availability unverified.
