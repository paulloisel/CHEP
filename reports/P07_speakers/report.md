## Findings and scholarly case

### Build from demonstrated interests

The institutional map supports a Paris-centred approach. CHEP is based at Sciences Po; its homepage distinguishes a collaboration beginning in 2019 from establishment in 2022. The three centres collaborate, but affiliation with Harvard or Cambridge alone does not create Paris membership. This distinction matters when proposing respondents: a locally based colleague and an overseas network participant present different practical requirements. [CHEP home](https://www.histecon.fr/en/), [Sciences Po CHEP overview](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/research/centre-for-history-and-economics-in-paris-chep/).

The strongest connections are methodological as well as thematic. Notarial materials offer an entry into transactions and legal classifications; debt history asks who finances collective projects; environmental history tests what economic measures omit. CHEP’s **Archives Notariales** provides a concrete local resource, while Lemercier and Trivellato’s comparative article illustrates how historians can compare records without assuming that categories mean the same thing everywhere. Those connections make a source workshop more useful than a succession of general book presentations. [Archives Notariales](https://www.histecon.fr/archivesnotariales/), [1751 and Thereabout](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/social-science-history/article/1751-and-thereabout-a-quantitative-and-comparative-approach-to-notarial-records/CBDDC9D2134379765F54476F66364227).

There is no evidence here of an unmet demand for additional meetings. Recent programming already addresses climate risk, finitude, forests, commodities, trust and accounting. An apparent omission from the checked pages is at most a lead for discussion with convenors. The added value proposed here is an explicit comparison exercise across sessions: identify the institution, its evidence, the people bearing costs and the limits of the historian’s inference. That design is our recommendation, not an established CHEP practice or an evaluated intervention. [Current and earlier Histoires programmes](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/).

### Why these eight

The shortlist has two linked purposes: four possible pilot contributions and four substantive alternatives. **Calafat** provides a Paris-based possibility for examining jurisdiction and commercial claims; **van Waijenburg** changes the scale to colonial fiscal measurement; **Jenkins** connects financing institutions to unequal urban outcomes; **Chatterjee** extends the conversation to energy-dependent social groups. **Dermineur** supplies credit networks and source reconstruction; **Murphy** offers institutional microhistory; **Farber** adds insurance and maritime governance; **Barca** opens a labour-and-care perspective on environmental costs. The [candidate briefs](shortlist.md) cite the particular scholarship, the material actually read and a question for each. None is recommended simply because of institutional standing.

Complementarity is qualified. Calafat was listed twice in the CHEP-supported Histoires series in 2023. Chatterjee’s 2022 **La matière du politique** contribution concerned energy protest; the proposed session would revisit that research through its published comparative framework, rather than advertise a wholly new subject. If convenors judge the overlap too close, Barca is the proposed substitute. Ogle and Warde also have documented joint-centre listings and remain useful reserves, returning/network contributors rather than proposed first appearances. Announcements verify that people were scheduled; they do not prove attendance or a completed event. [Histoires](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/histoires-de-la-vie-economique/), [La matière du politique](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/recherche/seminaires/seminaire-des-axes-et-des-domaines/la-matiere-du-politique/), [Joint online archive](https://histecon.fas.harvard.edu/seminars/past_seminars.html).

Career coverage uses documented positions rather than guesses from age or photographs. The pool includes assistant professors and researcher/fellow appointments alongside established professors. The junior-researcher reserve Pompermaier needs further direct verification of rank and article access; Vettese’s fellowship is time-limited and should be rechecked before any future programme. The shortlist spans early modern and modern history, with Mediterranean, African, Indian, European and US cases. Latin America and East Asia appear more strongly among reserves, and medieval history is absent. This is a limitation of the selected conversation, not a judgment about their importance. See the institution and research fields in [candidates.csv](candidates.csv).

## Selection rubric and use of the longlist

Apply five qualitative tests. **Research fit:** does the proposed question connect to documented CHEP work? **Specificity:** is there an identifiable work and a bounded issue rather than a generic theme? **Complementarity:** how does the contribution differ from, or deliberately revisit, recent programming? **Student learning:** can participants practise an explicit historical skill using accessible material? **Feasibility:** what academic, administrative, travel and access support is still needed? The first four determine scholarly suitability. Feasibility remains a separate assessment and can change the format without downgrading the scholarship.

The [selection matrix](selection_matrix.csv) records `strong`, `moderate` or `conditional` judgments with reasons, plus logistical unknowns; no arithmetic total is used. A shortlist place means there is enough evidence to propose a conversation. A reserve may be equally compelling academically but overlap with a selected case, depend on more reading, or serve a different geographical balance. The pilot order follows an argument, not a rank. Academic review should be able to swap a speaker while retaining the learning objective.

For example, an unavailable international speaker need not trigger a search for another prominent name. The convenor can retain the question and discuss a publicly accessible text, explicitly presenting it as a reading session without the author. Conversely, a Paris affiliation reduces likely travel complexity but establishes neither free participation nor willingness. Farber’s current profile explicitly notes leave in autumn 2026 and spring 2027; it does not specify her location or rule out participation. [Farber’s Columbia profile](https://history.columbia.edu/person/farber-hannah/).

The most feasible respondent pool begins with documented Paris-based work. Levy’s scholarship on capitalism and risk provides another relevant option. These are inferred intellectual matches, and none implies a commitment or a personal relationship. There is one especially useful distinction: Calafat and Trivellato are documented coauthors of the proposed article. Trivellato could therefore be considered as a co-presenter, but an independent respondent would ask different questions. The pilot instead suggests Todd locally; a second respondent is optional, since adding discussants also adds preparation and coordination. [The Shipwreck of the Turks, IAS repository](https://albert.ias.edu/entities/publication/6c55edbd-b125-4643-bc4b-f7592c1d302d).

The student should manage the reading sheet, source links, accessibility check and anonymous learning summary. A proposed academic convenor owns the question, invitations, discussion and respondent choice; administrative support handles approved bookings and reimbursement. Public-facing drafts remain editable until an authorised institutional sender approves them. The two invitation drafts deliberately leave dates, honoraria, travel and sender blank. No invitation, mailing-list subscription, application or publication was undertaken.

The proposed success criterion is scholarly usefulness rather than attendance alone. At each session, ask participants anonymously which comparison or source problem they can use and what needs clarification. Aggregate responses without individual tracking. If two sessions attract too little participation to sustain discussion, or require more than five student hours per week after rescheduling work, pause the external programme and retain a smaller text discussion. These thresholds are proposed operational rules, not observed demand.

## Methods, decisions and limitations

Summary.md was read and supplied the four expected institutional starting points. No other workstream report was required or used. Research followed official navigation and targeted name/topic searches, checked current institutional profiles, and read publisher descriptions, abstracts and available author essays. Full-book reading is not claimed. Twenty candidate records and eight briefs are supported by the source register; `evidence_read` exposes uneven access. Source IDs join claims to direct URLs, dates and retrieval status. Programme listings, publication dates, access dates and crawl ages are kept distinct.

Fresh official profiles take precedence over old book biographies. For example, Bridges is listed as a Senior Fellow at Vanderbilt’s Policy Accelerator; the publisher still describes a Harvard fellowship which Vanderbilt calls previous. The current role is recorded without treating the stale biography as a second current appointment. Calafat’s October 2023 event has conflicting dates across official calendars; the responsible seminar page is preferred while the discrepancy is retained. [Bridges at Vanderbilt](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/vanderbilt-policy-accelerator/mary-bridges/), [publisher biography](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691248387/dollars-and-dominion), [regional archive](https://histecon.fr/fr/archive.html).

Most retrievals used indexed/cached web content. Some official routes returned timeouts, 403 responses or empty dynamic pages; this does not establish that the live sites are broken. The evidence cutoff is 15 November 2026, not a guarantee of same-day site state. The scoped appearance check covers the available Paris programmes and joint archive, not every workshop participant or private invitation. `coverage.md` supplies the source tree, exclusions and remaining retrieval queue. Internal demand, invitation history, available dates, access needs, willingness, actual travel location and budgets remain unresolved. Those are the minimum practical questions for turning a researched shortlist into a supported programme.
