# Three celebration options and a minimum viable programme

**as_of_date: 2026-11-15. All formats, participants and dates below are proposals. Nothing has been booked, sent or published.** Planning scenario: five student hours per week and no confirmed discretionary budget. Monetary amounts are internal planning allowances in EUR, not supplier quotations or known CHEP expenditure; staff time and existing institutional resources are separately identified as in-kind dependencies.

## Why a reading-led digital retrospective fits

CHEP’s [5 June 2019 launch workshop report, *A Spatial Turn in Economic History?*](https://histecon.fr/en/spatial.html) documents short opening remarks, circulated questions and materials, and a shared bibliography (P11-M005; accessed 2026-11-15). This provides a local precedent for using a publication retrospective to start scholarly discussion. A public link-only bibliography also respects the historical report’s distinction between bibliographic references and PDFs that could not be freely disseminated. The [organisation page](https://histecon.fr/en/organisation.html) identifies coordination and web functions (P11-M010; accessed 2026-11-15); their capacity to support this proposal remains unconfirmed.

## Preferred version: A, prepared in six five-hour weeks

The digital object contains the anniversary note; opening essay; four thematic introductions; six full publication cards; the wider 12–18-item editorial selection; downloadable bibliography; short chronology; and explicit affiliation and rights labels. It uses text and links, with no covers or archival photographs. Readers can move from a theme to a work and then to a publisher, repository or library record. Retain French titles and supply short English orientation; an optional French 100–150-word invitation/summary should receive language review rather than imply that the entire edition is bilingual.

| Week | Student work | Hours | Reviewable result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Reconcile anniversary wording and record outstanding institutional questions; agree editorial scope. | 5 | One-page scope sheet and milestone decision log. |
| 2 | Recheck bibliography identifiers, editions and affiliation labels; prioritise junior and non-book records in the remaining queue. | 5 | Clean bibliography and transparent omissions list. |
| 3 | Edit opening, thematic connections and six cards; verify that each argument rests on inspected text or abstract. | 5 | Full text-only reading draft. |
| 4 | Incorporate one academic review; check permissions/status, link labels and accessibility. | 5 | Fact-checked candidate edition. |
| 5 | Prepare the accessible web/file package, source register and archive naming; hand off to authorised publisher. | 5 | Publication-ready package, subject to institutional decision. |
| 6, optional | Prepare discussion sheet (1.5 h), logistics and approved announcement draft (1 h), delivery/set-up (1.25 h), revise and archive questions (1.25 h). | 5 | 45-minute conversation and concise anonymous question note. |

## Concrete optional discussion programme: “What makes economic life visible?”

**Audience proposal:** CHEP-connected researchers and students, with access and capacity determined by the host. **Contributors:** academic chair, two readers from different career stages, and the student editor; all hypothetical. No named scholar is assigned a commitment. Supply the reading packet seven days beforehand if the event is approved. Participants need read only two short cards and their linked abstracts, not entire books.

| Minutes | Activity | Student contribution / output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Chair distinguishes the 2019 launch from the 2022 establishment and explains the publication corpus. | Show a three-date timeline and define “featured” versus “supported.” |
| 5–11 | Student presents two publication pairings across the thematic sections. | Explain a source/method connection; explicitly label this as editorial comparison, not evidence of influence. |
| 11–19 | Two volunteer readers offer four-minute responses. | Provide one common question in advance; no new formal paper required. |
| 19–36 | Open conversation in French or English, with chair ensuring brief clarification when needed. | Keep a non-attributed list of research questions and useful references; no recording by default. |
| 36–42 | Participants identify missing forms or perspectives. | Collect suggested articles, chapters, student work and public essays for verification, without promising inclusion. |
| 42–45 | Chair closes with one feasible continuation. | State correction route and archive date; no new recurring programme assumed. |

Discussion prompts: **How do family records, institutional archives and quantitative series make different economic actors visible? When does comparison across places clarify economic power, and when does it conceal institutional differences? What changes when a work crosses languages or appears as a short public essay? Which student contribution could change this reading list rather than merely add another name?** These are proposed editorial questions, not assertions that the authors influenced one another.

## Dissemination, rights and evaluation

After an institutional publication decision, a communications/web function could post the approved edition on CHEP’s existing site, make one announcement through its established channels, and invite Cambridge/Harvard counterparts to link to it if appropriate. The student prepares the text, captions and link package; the responsible function controls release. No messages or invitations are sent in this research run. Archive the dated PDF/HTML export and UTF-8 CSVs together; include access dates, revision history, named correction function and a clear freeze date. Do not upload full copyrighted texts. Cover images, photographs and quotations require item-level rights checks; the baseline avoids them.

Use modest learning measures: every showcased record has a direct source and relationship label; at least two students or early-career researchers review usability if willing; the optional discussion produces three usable research questions; and suggested omissions are either verified or explicitly left pending. These are proposed thresholds, not established demand or outcomes. Review after the first month: continue only if an academic sponsor and maintenance owner remain available and readers report concrete use. Otherwise archive the corrected edition and defer print or the half-day format. Reserve the monthly half-hour by reducing other student tasks; it is not free capacity.
