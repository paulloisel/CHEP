## Decisions, conflicts, and limits

The checked book years are 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025; no 2026 edition of these six works was established. This does not establish that none exists. French translations count as editions of the same underlying work. The author’s response is a new work, not a new book edition. Exact edition days are withheld when only month or year is supported by an opened source.

Le Bras’s fellowship timing is **conflicting**: CHEP’s past-fellows page says 2021–2023; its home page says since 2023; the publisher of his January 2024 book describes a CHEP postdoctoral role. The book’s date and title are verified, but a verified “published during affiliation” classification is not assigned. Génard’s fellowship is documented in 2021–2022, outside the 2025 publication year. Levy’s current title does not establish the start date of his CHEP role. For the other authors the featured-publication relationship is sufficient for inclusion without converting Harvard, Cambridge or Sciences Po membership into Paris membership.

The Princeton 2019 catalogue gives January for Trivellato’s book while the current indexed ebook page gives 12 February. Year 2019 is retained; exact release date remains unresolved and may reflect advance versus format-specific publication. Princeton pages sometimes repeat the selected edition’s details under several format headings; no such repetition was treated as reliable independent format metadata. Rothschild’s audio day was not transferred to the hardcover. The CNRS page’s 4 January 2024 publication field is used rather than a 2023 date inside one press quotation. Direct source retrieval failures are not claims that websites are broken.

No full book was read. Five cards draw on publisher descriptions, author-supplied presentation/contents, or author interview/project material; Trivellato’s additionally uses an actual publisher-hosted introduction. All connections between books are labelled editorial. No funding acknowledgement was found in this checked source set; **not_found_publicly** does not mean no support existed. Covers and archival images have not been cleared for reproduction. The draft therefore uses text and links only.

Next retrievals: obtain accessible publisher/title-page confirmation for exact French release dates and translator spelling for Trivellato; inspect the specific editions’ acknowledgements before any support claim; reconcile Le Bras’s dates with the responsible institutional function; determine documented affiliation intervals for Levy, Rothschild, Todd and Trivellato if an affiliation-period total is required. Full scholarly assessment would require reading the books or requesting concise author corrections later, neither represented as completed here. No outreach occurred.
