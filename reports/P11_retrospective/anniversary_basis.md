# Anniversary basis: establishment, launch and preparation cutoff

**as_of_date: 2026-11-15. Preparation draft; no anniversary event or publication is confirmed.** All linked sources were accessed on 15 November 2026; source IDs resolve in `sources.csv` (working component: `sources_milestones.csv`).

## Recommended framing

Use **“Five years since CHEP’s establishment, 2022–2027: histories of economic life”**, subject to institutional confirmation. Add a visible subtitle: **“Preparation draft, evidence checked through 15 November 2026; with a prehistory of collaboration and launch in 2019.”** Do not call this “CHEP’s first five years” without qualification: the centre itself describes a launch in June 2019 as well as establishment in 2022.

The [CHEP English home](https://www.histecon.fr/en/) (P11-M001), [French home](https://histecon.fr/fr/) (P11-M002), and [Sciences Po CHEP overview](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/research/centre-for-history-and-economics-in-paris-chep/) (P11-M003) distinguish collaboration beginning in 2019 from establishment in 2022. The dated [Sciences Po announcement of 2026 research grants](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ecole-recherche/fr/actualites/quatre-jeunes-chercheurs-histoire-laureats-bourse-chep-2026/) (28 January 2026; P11-M004) independently repeats this sequence. These sources establish years, not a day or month of formal establishment.

## Exact editorial coverage dates

- **Evidence access and preparation cutoff:** 2026-11-15, inclusive. No 2027 publication or event is represented as accomplished.
- **Main establishment-era publication window for this draft:** 2022-01-01 to 2026-11-15, inclusive. January 1 is an editorial calendar boundary, **not** a claimed founding date.
- **Collaboration prehistory:** 2019-01-01 to 2021-12-31, inclusive; distinguish dated launch and event records from works merely published in those years.
- **Earlier contextual works:** retain separately when needed to identify an original work behind a later translation or a title featured by CHEP. They do not count as establishment-era outputs.
- **Proposed final edition:** freeze at an institutionally agreed date in 2027, still to be decided. A label such as “2022–2027” identifies the commemorated interval, not six completed calendar years of evidence or exactly five elapsed years from January 1.

## What resolves the remaining uncertainty?

The academic coordinator and host’s administrative/archive function should confirm: **“Should the celebration mark the 5 June 2019 launch or the formal establishment recorded as 2022; and, for the latter, which dated agreement or institutional decision defines the anniversary day?”** A launch programme, signed agreement, council decision or dated institutional announcement would settle the exact date. A 2027 event can safely be described as marking five years since the **2022 establishment** even if only the year is confirmed; avoid an invented birthday.
