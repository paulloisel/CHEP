### DRAFT — Research in progress: four 2026 recipients

**Francesco Giosuè — Liberalism across borders.** Giosuè studies the transnational formation of liberalism between the Atlantic Revolutions and the Restoration. His project follows links between the Monarchiens and the Doctrinaires and the political and intellectual exchanges made possible by emigration. The research brings networks of sociability into the history of political ideas. His CHSP doctorate began in September 2025. [“Francesco Giosuè,” Sciences Po CHSP](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/annuaire/giosue-francesco/), P10-S318.

**Rébecca Mathiot — Working at sea, claiming protection.** Mathiot examines occupational risks among French fishermen and merchant seafarers, from the late nineteenth century to the 1950s. Her thesis connects prevention, inspection, medical care and compensation to a history of dangerous work. It asks how maritime occupations can illuminate the margins of social protection. She began her CHSP doctorate in September 2025. [“Rébecca Mathiot,” Sciences Po CHSP](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/annuaire/mathiot-rebecca/), P10-S315.

**Giulio Piumelli — How an economic consensus was made.** Piumelli investigates the creation and implementation of the Washington Consensus, 1979–1995. His research examines relations among US officials, international financial institutions and indebted countries, asking how deliberate policy choices interacted with contingent pressures. The project treats debtor–creditor relations as a historical problem rather than assuming a single direction of power. His Sciences Po cotutelle began in 2024. [“Giulio Piumelli,” Sciences Po CHSP](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/directory/piumelli-giulio/), P10-S316.

**Aesa Virely — Care beyond productive work.** Virely studies children’s homes and homes for older people in socialist Yugoslavia, 1945–1991. Her project brings childhood and old age into a shared history of vulnerability and protection, asking how a society that valorised productive labour cared for people unable to work because of their age. Her CHSP profile records a doctorate begun in 2025. [“Aesa Virely,” Sciences Po CHSP](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/annuaire/virely-aesa/), P10-S317.
