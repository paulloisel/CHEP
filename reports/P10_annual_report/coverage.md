# P10 coverage, evidence decisions and remaining queue

**as_of_date / access date:** 2026-11-15. **Main period:** 2025-09-01–2026-08-31, inclusive. **Status:** internal audit trail for an annual-report prototype, not an exhaustive publication, people, funding or event census. Only P10 was executed. `Summary.md` was available and read; it supplied the four institutional starting URLs and no additional substantive narrative. No other P01–P12 report was used or awaited.

## Corpus and institutional boundaries

The corpus centres on Paris: CHEP navigation, its Sciences Po host, selected public research descriptions, publisher/repository records, dated programme pages and support announcements. Cambridge and Harvard enter only for documented Paris-linked events, shared programmes, and affiliation cross-checks. Their entire membership, publications, funds and event schedules are outside scope.

Affiliation timing is preserved. The current associate list is a discovery tool, not a record of membership throughout 2025–2026. Former-fellow achievements remain visibly historical relationships. Event speakers are not automatically members. A current research profile can explain a project but does not establish which findings were already available at the end of August; those version questions are retained for author review.

The brief's full shared method was applied with a focused editorial stopping rule: inspect the relevant navigation; extract complete rows from explicitly selected annual programmes and the 2026 award cohort; investigate selected research stories; document inaccessible branches and unprocessed bibliography leads. The result is sufficient for a reviewable prototype, not a claim of complete coverage.

## Reconciliation and consequential choices

1. **Reporting period:** no CHEP-specific convention was located. Follow the selected prompt's 1 September–31 August rule. A discovered institutional annual report covers all Sciences Po, not a documented CHEP reporting requirement. Targeted searches included `site:histecon.fr "annual report"`, `site:histecon.fr "rapport"`, `site:histecon.fr "2026" "2025"`, and `site:sciencespo.fr/histoire "CHEP" "2026"`.
2. **Coordinator:** newer English CHEP and host sources name Levy; much older French indexed content names Todd. Use Coordinator for Levy, record the discrepancy and leave transition dates unknown. Retrieval age is not the page's publication date or proof of the current live French wording.
3. **Book date:** Génard's signed 12 September 2025 announcement establishes appearance announced within period, not the exact first release. It is excluded from the strict publication indicator pending confirmation. BnF ISBN evidence is indexed-only, explicitly queried. Levy's February/April 2025 editions are prior-period context.
4. **Journal date:** ENS supplies 20 November 2025 print publication and HAL corroborates contributions. ENS cached content still says forthcoming; journal indexed online date 1 October was not directly inspected because of an access challenge. Use the labelled publisher print date, retain the discrepancy and do not imply verified physical distribution on that day.
5. **Junior conference:** detailed event page and PDF say 27–28 November 2025; index says 26–27. Count one conference, provisionally use the detailed dates, and query the final programme. A changed keynote/roundtable listing means no claim that a named keynote speaker actually attended.
6. **Shared activity:** three joint online events are not three additional solely Paris-organised events. Eight contextual marine/environmental sessions are outside the fourteen-record core/shared denominator. Centre support for a series does not establish session-specific payments.
7. **Career chronology:** host dates Skoglund's invited postdoctoral role from December 2025 while the author's current page reports May 2026 PhD. Use “visiting researcher”; keep dates of the documented visit, without asserting a degree throughout it. Current abstract version requires author confirmation for year-end description.
8. **Rosters and carryover:** Chaker's undated current host listing is held outside period-specific claims. Past fellowship and current associate listings remain separate. February 2025 awards could have supported later activity, but this was not established; do not add the earlier cohort automatically. Likewise, a 2027 call is not a 2026 award.
9. **Standing resources:** the notarial portal and 2024–2025 reading group are context, not verified new 2025–2026 outputs. Seminar opportunities are not formal training completions; an international workshop is not a count of funded mobility visits.
