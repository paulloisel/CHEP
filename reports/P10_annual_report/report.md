## Purpose, audience and editorial choices

The annual report should answer a reader's practical questions: what research is being pursued, where can it be read, how do people contribute, and which activities connect the community? Members need accurate credit; prospective students need approachable examples of research and participation; partners need a clear account of shared work; supporters need an honest account of support. These are proposed editorial uses, not measured reader demand. A short pilot should test them before a larger publication is commissioned.

The institutional starting point is specific. CHEP is based at the Sciences Po Centre for History; collaboration began in 2019 and establishment followed in 2022. The English organisation page and Sciences Po identify Jonathan Levy as **Coordinator**. The retrieved French organisation page names David Todd but is a much older indexed state. This supports using the newer corroborated wording while querying the historical handover, not declaring the live French site defective. [CHEP overview](https://www.histecon.fr/en/), P10-S002; [host page](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/en/research/centre-for-history-and-economics-in-paris-chep/), P10-S004; [Organisation](https://histecon.fr/en/organisation.html), P10-S001; [French version](https://histecon.fr/fr/organisation.html), P10-S006.

No CHEP-specific annual reporting convention was established in the checked navigation and targeted searches. Accordingly, the latest completed September–August year is used. A whole-Sciences-Po annual report surfaced in discovery, but it does not establish CHEP's own reporting practice. [Coverage and search record](coverage.md). Material after 31 August 2026 is separated in the prototype's postscript; older scholarship can illuminate current work only when labelled background.

The narrative uses three conversations: authority in everyday life; property and emancipation; environmental transformation and social costs. Génard's prison history supplies a published-work feature, while Balguy's colonial-land research and Skoglund's Landes project demonstrate ongoing inquiry. These choices mix institutional/social history, economic and environmental questions, archival reconstruction and quantitative evidence. The selection is based on readable descriptions and dated Paris connections, not presumed prestige. [Author's book description](https://www.asso-h2c.fr/2025/09/12/parution-elsa-genard-sous-les-verrous-une-histoire-sociale-de-la-prison/), P10-S105; [Balguy profile](https://www.sciencespo.fr/histoire/fr/annuaire/balguy-jessica/), P10-S016; [Skoglund abstracts](https://linaskoglund.github.io/), P10-S015.

The four doctoral profiles supply a further career-stage and subject range. Their public research descriptions support concise accounts, but not claims that grants caused particular results. The draft contains no invented interviews, quotations, testimonials or foreword. Its images are deliberately absent because reproduction rights were not established. [CHEP student roster](https://histecon.fr/en/research_students.html), P10-S302; profile sources P10-S315–318.

## Minimum edition and repeatable production

The six-page minimum keeps the overview, three compact features, junior section, programme/support account and selected reading list. It combines the last two spreads and reduces contextual prose. Start with accessible web text; add a PDF only when there is capacity to render and verify it. A short French summary and preserved original French titles provide a manageable initial language option. Full translation is a separate estimated 10–16 hours plus 3–4 review hours, outside the baseline. No paid design, printing, photographs or licensing is assumed.

The [workflow](editorial_workflow.md) includes an eight-question contribution form, proposed calendar and ownership by function. Source records feed the content register; stable item IDs feed the indicators. A monthly half-hour capture avoids reconstructing the year from changing homepages. Six production weeks at four student hours each leave room for external review; archival work closes the cycle. Record actual hours and corrections to test the estimates. Success means a usable, correctly credited edition delivered within capacity, not a higher publication count.

Before release, institutional review must settle the eight explicit queries: reporting/role credits; book release date; Balguy's annual milestones; Skoglund's research-version and career chronology; doctoral descriptions at year-end; event delivery and conference dates; bibliographic additions; and permissible support statements. The junior conference index and detailed programme disagree on dates; the draft uses 27–28 November provisionally. Skoglund's host role wording and author degree date differ, so the public draft uses “visiting researcher”. These are targeted questions for later review, not reasons to invent missing copy or postpone the prototype. [Queries in draft](annual_report_draft.md); source comparisons in [coverage.md](coverage.md).

## Methods and limits

Summary.md was read and contained four starting URLs, with no additional narrative. The shared source map was expanded independently for P10; no other report was required or used. Navigation, language variants, period-labelled programmes, publisher/repository records and official profiles were inspected. Research features use accessible abstracts, author-supplied descriptions and institutional research summaries; metadata-only work receives bibliographic notice.

Coverage is deliberately focused. Eighteen entries on the selected publication list were read at listing level, but eleven publisher leads remain unprocessed and the entire affiliate bibliography was not searched. Event landing pages and Cambridge programme targets had retrieval failures; targeted official sources recovered useful material without establishing exhaustive coverage. Cached/indexed source ages are recorded, never treated as event or publication dates. No dated archival snapshots were used. The unavailable robots/sitemap and local DNS failure ruled out a bulk crawl; access restrictions were respected. [Coverage tree and remaining queue](coverage.md); [source register](sources.csv).

The package supports a concrete editorial decision and reviewable first edition. It cannot establish demand, satisfaction, annual membership, actual spending or institutional approval. Those questions require the nominated functions and limited contributor review, with data collection confined to what the annual report genuinely needs.
